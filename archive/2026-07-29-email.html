STN: Saturn Metals raises $100m at $0.40 to fund Apollo Hill construction readiness

Saturn Metals secured firm commitments for a $100 million two-tranche placement at $0.40 per share, an 11.1% discount to last close of $0.450. New cornerstone Golden Crane Holdings is committing approximately $48.6 million for 121.5 million shares, expected to reach roughly 15% of the company. A non-underwritten $5 million SPP follows, with Tranche Two's $75 million requiring shareholder approval on 17 September 2026. Funds target front end engineering, long lead items and construction readiness at Apollo Hill, following a 26% resource upgrade to 2.83Moz.

 
TOK: Tolu Minerals extends high-grade hits at Tolukuma ahead of August MRE

Tolu Minerals reported assays from 17 new diamond holes at Tolukuma, headlined by 3.9m at 42.35g/t Au from 84.1m (TUD-26-013) and 2.0m at 32.01g/t Au from 20.0m plus 1.0m at 37.54g/t Au from 149.0m (TSD-26-030). The revised geological model now recognises 44 mineralised veins and splays, up from 15, lifting interpreted vein volume by about 15%. Results feed an updated Mineral Resource Estimate due late August 2026, ahead of targeted first production in Q1 2027.

 
AS1: Asara Resources hits high-grade gold in maiden southern fence drilling at Massan

Asara Resources returned 47m at 8.0g/t Au from 10m (MSRC26-167), including 8m at 42.2g/t Au, plus 66m at 0.7g/t Au from 84m (MSRC26-154) and 19m at 2.1g/t Au from 105m (MSRC26-151), from first-pass drilling south of the Massan centre at Kada. Hole MSRC26-167 also returned two consecutive 1m intervals grading over 130g/t Au. Results confirm mineralisation extending into a previously under-drilled southern extension ahead of an August 2026 MRE update.

 
PGO: Pacgold halted pending capital raising announcement

Pacgold is halted pending an announcement regarding a capital raising, with trading to resume the earlier of normal trading on Friday 31 July 2026 or release of the announcement.

 
GA8: GoldArc retracts undisclosed rock chip samples from Yttria announcement

GoldArc Resources retracted three rock chip samples reported in its 27 July 2026 announcement on the Yttria Aircore Project after failing to disclose information required under ASX Listing Rules 5.6 and 5.7. The company says the samples are surface reconnaissance material not required to support the drilling results, and the drilling results themselves remain unchanged.

Day in Brief
 

The most material development was Saturn Metals' strongly supported $100 million placement at $0.40 per share, an 11.1% discount, bringing in cornerstone investor Golden Crane Holdings for roughly 15% of the company to fund Apollo Hill construction readiness. Elsewhere, drilling news dominated: Tolu Minerals reported 3.9m at 42.35g/t Au among further high-grade Tolukuma intercepts ahead of a late-August resource update, and Asara Resources returned 47m at 8.0g/t Au including 8m at 42.2g/t Au from newly drilled southern fence lines at Kada. Toubani Resources' quarterly confirmed a A$302 million funding package (US$160m gold stream plus A$70m equity) to complete Kobada construction, now 61% committed. Pacgold remains halted pending a capital raising announcement, and GoldArc retracted undisclosed rock chip data from its Yttria announcement without affecting the underlying drilling results.
