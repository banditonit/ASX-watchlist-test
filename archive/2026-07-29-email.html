STN: Saturn Metals secures $100 million to advance Apollo Hill

Saturn Metals completed a two-tranche placement raising $100 million (before costs) at $0.40 per share, an 11.1% discount to last close of $0.450. Tranche One (63.8 million shares, approximately $25 million) proceeds under existing 7.1 and 7.1A capacity settles 6 August 2026; Tranche Two (186.2 million shares, approximately $75 million) requires shareholder approval at a general meeting on 17 September 2026. New cornerstone investor Golden Crane Holdings has committed approximately $48.6 million for 121.5 million shares, expected to hold approximately 15% of the company post-raise, with settlement of part of its position subject to FIRB approval. A non-underwritten Share Purchase Plan targeting a further $5 million at the same price opens 6 August and closes 21 August 2026. Funds are earmarked for front end engineering and design, long lead item procurement, construction readiness, statutory approvals, site establishment and exploration drilling, following a 26% increase in Apollo Hill's resource to 2.83Moz (2.19Moz Measured and Indicated), execution of the Native Title Mining and Project Agreement and grant of Mining Lease M31/496. Petra Capital acted as sole lead manager, bookrunner and underwriter; Canaccord Genuity acted as co-lead manager.

 
PGO: Pacgold halted pending capital raising announcement

Pacgold requested a trading halt effective immediately pending an announcement regarding a capital raising. The halt is to remain in place until the earlier of the commencement of normal trading on Friday, 31 July 2026, or release of the announcement.

 
TOK: Tolu Minerals reports further high-grade Tolukuma intercepts ahead of MRE

Tolu Minerals received assays for 17 diamond drill holes at Tolukuma, headlined by 3.9m at 42.35g/t Au from 84.1m (TUD-26-013), 2.0m at 32.01g/t Au from 20.0m and 1.0m at 37.54g/t Au from 149.0m (TSD-26-030), 1.0m at 21.09g/t Au from 14.0m (TMLSDD0012) in a newly identified Gulbadi Splay, 1.2m at 12.80g/t Au from 126.0m (TSD-26-034), and 3.0m at 4.67g/t Au from 123.6m (TUD-26-021). The results feed into an updated Mineral Resource Estimate on track for late August 2026. The company's revised geological model now recognises 44 mineralised veins and splays, up from 15, increasing interpreted mineralised vein volume by approximately 15%. 143 drill holes for 22,975m have been completed to 30 June 2026 with seven diamond rigs operating and approximately 75,000m planned, ahead of a targeted first production by Q1 2027.

 
AS1: Asara delivers strong first results from southern Massan fence lines

Asara Resources reported first assay results from newly drilled southern fence lines at the Massan Deposit (Kada Gold Project, Guinea), including 47m at 8.0g/t Au from 10m in MSRC26-167 (including 8m at 42.2g/t Au from 11m), 66m at 0.7g/t Au from 84m in MSRC26-154 (including 3m at 3.8g/t Au), and 19m at 2.1g/t Au from 105m in MSRC26-151 (including 6m at 3.7g/t Au). MSRC26-167 also returned two consecutive 1m intervals grading over 130g/t Au, complementing a prior >150g/t Au over 1m result approximately 500m south along strike in MSRC26-041. A total of 57 holes for 7,146m have been completed and reported as part of ongoing resource growth drilling, with results to be incorporated ahead of an internal cut-off for the updated August 2026 MRE.

 
SKM: Skylark confirms broad M'Basso mineralisation, acquires Vavoua

Skylark Minerals completed Phase 1 RC drilling at the M'Basso Prospect (Zaranou Gold Project, Cote d'Ivoire), comprising 38 holes for 6,667m, confirming two coherent mineralised zones over an extended strike of approximately 750m. Best results included 62m at 1.41g/t Au from 114m in ZARC0148 (including 23m at 2.41g/t Au from 153m and 4m at 6.99g/t Au from 161m), 24m at 3.07g/t Au from 233m in ZARC0143 (including 1m at 26.52g/t Au from 243m), and 34m at 1.30g/t Au from 45m in ZARC0140. Metallurgical testwork on 16 samples returned average LeachWELL gold extraction of approximately 95% (range 89% to 99%). Phase 2 step-out drilling has commenced with eight holes completed and assays pending. Separately, Skylark exercised its option to acquire 100% of the Vavoua Gold Project in central Cote d'Ivoire for A$150,000 cash consideration. Cash at 30 June 2026 was A$4.85 million, with directors and management purchasing 5,375,604 shares for approximately A$1.36 million between 1 April and 16 July 2026.

Day in Brief
 

The most material development was Saturn Metals' strongly supported $100 million two-tranche placement at $0.40 per share to advance the Apollo Hill Gold Project, bringing in cornerstone investor Golden Crane Holdings for approximately $48.6 million (circa 15% of the company) alongside continued substantial support from its four largest existing shareholders (a combined $42.5 million), with proceeds earmarked for front end engineering, long lead items and construction readiness ahead of a Definitive Feasibility Study due later this calendar year. Elsewhere, Pacgold entered a trading halt pending a capital raising announcement, while three explorers, Tolu Minerals, Asara Resources and Skylark Minerals, delivered high-grade drilling updates feeding into resource estimates due in August, and Santana Minerals and Toubani Resources reported quarterlies dominated by construction and funding progress at their respective gold and Kobada projects.
