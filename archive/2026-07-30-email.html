HRN: Horizon Gold: Gum Creek DFS delivers maiden 728koz reserve and A$1.3B NPV

Horizon's June quarterly confirms the Gum Creek Open Pit DFS, delivering a maiden Ore Reserve of 18.2Mt at 1.24g/t Au for 728koz, average production of 98kozpa over the first five years and 880koz over a 10-year mine life. Pre-production capital is A$350 million, with pre-tax NPV of A$1,307 million, 53.1% IRR and AISC of A$2,995/oz at a A$5,500/oz gold price. Fresh diamond and RC results at Omega, Kingfisher, Swift and Eagle included 14m at 2.87g/t Au from 337m (Omega) and 6m at 5.13g/t Au from 95m (Swift, SFRC002). The company completed a $30 million raise and held $24.0 million cash at quarter end, targeting FID in Q2 2027.

 
LGM: Legacy Minerals: Mt Carrington resource grows 151% since acquisition to 1.6Moz gold-equivalent

Legacy Minerals lifted the Mt Carrington open-pit Mineral Resource to 47.9Mt containing 1.6Moz gold-equivalent, including 714koz gold and 35Moz silver, a 39% tonnage increase and 69% jump in Indicated Resources since the 2025 estimate. Growth was driven by new copper and silver domains, updated metal prices and additional drilling. A high-grade component of 493koz AuEq at 3.1g/t AuEq sits within the estimate, and all deposits remain open along strike and at depth, feeding into an upcoming optimised Scoping Study.

 
MI6: Minerals 260: Bullabulling PFS confirms A$2.3B NPV and resource lifted to 6.2Moz

Minerals 260 completed a Stage 1 PFS at Bullabulling confirming a post-tax NPV of A$2.3 billion, 43% IRR and average annual production of about 150,000oz over the first ten years from a maiden Ore Reserve of 90Mt at 0.86g/t Au for 2.5Moz. The Mineral Resource was separately increased 38% to 190Mt at 1.0g/t Au for 6.2Moz. Early construction included a $59.1 million village contract with ATCO, and the company held approximately $211 million cash and deposits at quarter end, targeting FID in Q1 2027.

 
TCG: Turaco Gold: Afema PFS outlines 200,000ozpa producer with 1.91Moz reserve

Turaco's PFS for the Afema Project in Côte d'Ivoire confirmed a maiden Probable Ore Reserve of 55.1Mt at 1.1g/t for 1.91Moz, supporting average production of approximately 200,000oz per annum over a 10-year-plus mine life. Post-tax NPV5% is US$2.1 billion at a 79% IRR and 13-month payback, with initial development capital of US$410 million and AISC of US$1,508/oz. The Mineral Resource stands at 4.65Moz following infill drilling, and the company held approximately $51 million cash, targeting DFS completion within 12 months.

 
BTR: Brightstar Resources: Goldfields FID approved, construction ramping toward mid-2027 production

Brightstar's Board approved Final Investment Decision for the 100%-owned Goldfields Project, with all key regulatory approvals received and an EPC contract executed with GRES for the new 1.5Mtpa Laverton Processing Plant. The project is targeted to commence gold production in June 2027 at approximately 75kozpa over an initial six-year mine life. The company ended the quarter with $122 million cash and $161 million undrawn bond proceeds for total liquidity of $283 million, after closing out its Ocean Partners working capital facility.

 
BGD: Barton Gold: Tunkillia infill assays support PFS ahead of Q1 CY27 completion

Barton reported further Phase 2 RC infill assays at the Tunkillia Gold Project, including 29m at 1.24g/t Au from 48m (TKB0618) and 20m at 1.24g/t Au from 205m (TKB0621), infilling the Area 223 North and southern Area 223 pit zones. The Tunkillia PFS, led by GR Engineering, is targeting Q1 CY27 completion, with the underlying Optimised Scoping Study having outlined an NPV7.5% of approximately A$1.4 billion and 73.2% IRR. Further assays remain pending from the approximately 40,000m Phase 2 program.

 
LAC: Lac Gold: High-grade Rouyn intercepts point to resource growth, Golden Patricia mine acquired

Lac Gold reported strong drilling results at the Astoria deposit within its Rouyn Gold Project in Québec, including 5.65m at 141.29g/t Au from 148.15m (AS-26-793, including 0.50m at 1,580g/t Au) and 97.50m at 1.04g/t Au from 554.65m (AS-26-787). The results support a 1.66Moz gold Mineral Resource and an evolving interpretation of higher-grade shoots within broad envelopes. The company also completed the acquisition of the historic Golden Patricia Gold Mine from Barrick, and held $13.34 million cash at quarter end.

 
MBK: Metal Bank lodges Mining Lease application for Kingsley gold deposit

Metal Bank's subsidiary WesternX applied for a Mining Lease over the Kingsley deposit at the Livingstone Gold Project in WA, which hosts an Inferred Mineral Resource of 1.68Mt at 1.35g/t Au for 73,000oz. Previous preliminary metallurgical test work returned average recoveries of 93.8%. The application follows recent ML lodgements at the Homestead and Winja deposits, part of the company's pathway toward near-term gold production.

 
PDI: Predictive Discovery: Guinea refining decree adds compliance step, Kiniero ramp continues

Guinea's government issued a decree requiring domestic gold refining to 95.5% purity before export from 6 October 2026, prompting a temporary pause in exports before Predictive Discovery shipped approximately 33.8koz for provisional receipts of US$135.7 million. Kiniero poured 54,252oz at AISC of US$1,254/oz, well above nameplate capacity, while the group remains on track for 2026 guidance of 198,000-220,000oz at AISC of US$1,300-1,500/oz. Cash and bullion stood at US$364.3 million, and US$13.8 million in Guinea capital gains tax was agreed relating to the Robex merger.

Day in Brief
 

The most material development across the watchlist today is Horizon Gold's confirmation of the Gum Creek Open Pit DFS within its June quarterly, delivering a maiden Ore Reserve of 728koz gold underpinning average production of 98kozpa over the first five years, pre-tax NPV of A$1,307 million and a 53.1% IRR at A$5,500/oz gold, alongside continued high-grade drill results at Omega and Swift.
