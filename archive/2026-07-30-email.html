HRN: Horizon Gold: Gum Creek DFS underpins quarterly with A$350m development case

Horizon Gold's quarterly carried forward its Gum Creek Open Pit DFS, targeting average annual production of 98koz over the first five years and 880koz over a 10-year mine life, with first gold in H2 2028. Pre-production capital is A$350 million, pre-tax NPV is A$1,307 million and IRR 53.1% at A$5,500/oz gold, with AISC of A$2,995/oz. A maiden Ore Reserve of 18.2Mt at 1.24g/t Au for 728koz underpins the plan, alongside a 37.97Mt at 1.89g/t Au resource for 2.30Moz. FID is targeted for Q2 2027.

 
MI6: Minerals 260: Bullabulling PFS delivers A$2.3bn NPV, resource grows to 6.2Moz

Minerals 260's quarterly confirmed the Bullabulling PFS, which delivered a post-tax NPV of A$2.3 billion, IRR of 43% and average annual production of approximately 150,000oz over the initial 10 years. A maiden Ore Reserve of 90Mt at 0.86g/t Au for 2.5Moz was declared, while the Mineral Resource increased 38% to 190Mt at 1.0g/t Au for 6.2Moz. FID is targeted Q1 CY27, with the company holding roughly A$211 million cash and deposits.

 
TCG: Turaco Gold: Afema PFS confirms 200kozpa project with maiden 1.91Moz reserve

Turaco's quarterly confirmed the Afema PFS, outlining average annual production of approximately 200,000oz over a 10-year-plus mine life and a maiden JORC Probable Ore Reserve of 55.1Mt at 1.1g/t for 1.91Moz. The study delivered a post-tax NPV5% of US$2.1 billion, IRR of 79% and 13-month payback at US$3,500/oz gold, with initial development capital of US$410 million and AISC of US$1,508/oz. The Mineral Resource stands at 4.65Moz, and the company held cash of approximately $51 million at quarter end.

 
BTR: Brightstar Resources: Goldfields FID approved, Laverton mill under construction

Brightstar's quarterly confirmed the Board approved Final Investment Decision for the 100%-owned Goldfields Project, with all key regulatory approvals received and an EPC contract executed with GR Engineering for the new 1.5Mtpa Laverton Processing Plant. Gold production is targeted from June 2027 at approximately 75koz per annum over an initial six-year mine life. The company ended the quarter with $122 million cash and $161 million of undrawn net bond proceeds for total liquidity of $283 million.

 
LGM: Legacy Minerals: Mt Carrington resource grows 151% since 2024 acquisition to 1.6Moz gold-equivalent

Legacy Minerals lifted the Mt Carrington Mineral Resource Estimate to 47.9Mt containing 1.6Moz gold-equivalent, including 714koz gold and 35Moz silver, up 39% in tonnes since the 2025 estimate and 151% since the 2024 acquisition. Indicated Resources rose 69%, materially improving confidence ahead of an optimised Scoping Study. All deposits remain open along strike and at depth.

 
BGD: Barton Gold: Tunkillia infill assays support PFS as Phase 2 drilling nears completion

Barton Gold reported further assays from its roughly 40,000m Tunkillia Phase 2 upgrade drilling, infilling Area 223 North and the southern Area 223 pit. Key intercepts included 29m at 1.24g/t Au from 48m (including 4m at 4.08g/t Au) and 22m at 0.93g/t Au from 44m. The PFS, led by GR Engineering, targets Q1 CY27 completion, with a Mining Lease application to follow.

 
BC8: Black Cat Syndicate: Record Paulsens production; Barclays trading dispute filed

Black Cat's quarterly reported group gold production of 90,833oz for FY26, with Paulsens delivering a record 10,620oz in the June quarter on the back of the Regulus and Lynx discoveries. Quarterly gold sold was 21,660oz at an average realised price of $6,261/oz, generating record operating cashflow of $77 million. The company also disclosed it has commenced Supreme Court of WA proceedings against Barclays Capital Asia alleging breaches of Market Integrity Rules relating to trading in its shares.

 
RSG: Resolute Mining: ABC Project resource expanded to 3.0Moz; Doropo construction ramps up

Resolute's quarterly confirmed an expanded Inferred Mineral Resource of 133Mt at 0.71g/t Au for 3.0Moz at the ABC Project in Côte d'Ivoire, from over 31,000m of 2026 drilling. Group gold production was 45,192oz at AISC of $2,484/oz, with Doropo construction ramping up on a A$36.7 million quarterly spend and post-quarter local bank financing of $155 million secured toward the project.

 
PDI: Predictive Discovery: Guinea gold refining decree and capital gains tax settlement

Predictive Discovery's quarterly disclosed that Guinea's government issued a decree on 8 July 2026 requiring domestic gold refining to 95.5% purity before export, with a 90-day transition to 6 October 2026; the company has since shipped approximately 33.8koz under existing arrangements. Separately, PDI agreed to pay US$13.8 million in capital gains taxes and duties assessed in connection with its merger with Robex, receiving confirmation no further claims will arise in Guinea from the transaction.

 
AS1: Asara Resources: A$60m placement funds accelerated Kada drilling; new Chair and MD appointed

Asara Resources raised A$60 million at $0.125 per share to accelerate exploration at its Kada Gold Project in Guinea, alongside board changes including Jeff Quartermaine as Chairman and Matthew Sharples as Managing Director. Drilling at the Massan Deposit totalled 259 holes for 33,593m during the quarter, with high-grade intercepts including 82m at 2.2g/t Au from 5m including 5m at 6.9g/t Au. Cash on hand was $59.753 million at quarter end.

 
TTM: Titan Minerals: Cerro Verde trenching confirms extensions to Dynasty resource

Titan Minerals reported surface trenching results at the Cerro Verde prospect within its Dynasty Gold Project confirming a 400m extension to the west and 150m to the east of the current resource, including 6.1m at 5.1g/t Au and 3.5m at 3.9g/t Au. Three diamond rigs are now operating under a 10,000m program targeting the 3.9Moz gold, 26.1Moz silver MRE, with a Scoping Study expected in Q4 2026.

 
LAC: Lac Gold: high-grade Rouyn assays and Golden Patricia acquisition completed

Lac Gold reported strong assays from its Rouyn Gold Project including 5.65m at 141.29g/t Au (including 0.50m at 1,580g/t Au) and 97.50m at 1.04g/t Au, supporting growth potential beyond the existing 1.66Moz resource. The company also completed acquisition of the historic Golden Patricia Gold Mine from Barrick, which produced 619,796oz at 16.48g/t between 1988-1997, consolidating its Pickle Lake position. Cash on hand was A$13.34 million.

 
MBK: Metal Bank lodges Mining Lease application for Kingsley gold deposit

Metal Bank's subsidiary WesternX applied for a Mining Lease over the Kingsley deposit at the Livingstone Gold Project in WA, which hosts an Inferred Resource of 1.68Mt at 1.35g/t Au for 73,000oz. Previous metallurgical test work averaged 93.8% recovery. The application follows recent ML applications over the Homestead and Winja deposits at Livingstone and the Seven Leaders deposit at Whiteheads.

Day in Brief
 

The most material development came from Horizon Gold, whose quarterly reaffirmed the Gum Creek Open Pit DFS: average annual production of 98koz over the first five years (880koz over 10 years), pre-production capital of A$350 million, pre-tax NPV of A$1,307 million at 53.1% IRR (A$5,500/oz gold basis), and a maiden Ore Reserve of 18.2Mt at 1.24g/t Au for 728koz, with FID targeted Q2 2027. It was a heavy day for development-stage studies more broadly: Minerals 260's Bullabulling PFS delivered a A$2.3 billion post-tax NPV and lifted its resource 38% to 6.2Moz, while Turaco Gold's Afema PFS confirmed a 200kozpa, US$2.1 billion NPV project with a maiden 1.91Moz reserve. Brightstar Resources advanced from FID into construction at its Goldfields Project. On the corporate side, Predictive Discovery disclosed a new Guinea gold-refining decree requiring domestic processing from October 2026, alongside a US$13.8 million capital gains tax settlement tied to its Robex merger, while Black Cat Syndicate revealed Supreme Court proceedings against Barclays Capital Asia over share trading conduct. Quarterlies from Pantoro Gold, Kingsgate Consolidated and AuMEGA Metals rounded out a data-heavy session with no fresh guidance surprises.
