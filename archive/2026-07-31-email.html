BM1: Ballard tables conference deck, no new figures disclosed

Ballard Mining has released a corporate presentation ahead of the Diggers & Dealers Mining Forum in Kalgoorlie from 3 to 5 August 2026, with management taking investor meetings on site. The deck carries the company's "high grade gold, growing, permitted, drill and build" positioning but contains no new operational or financial figures beyond that already disclosed. Investors should watch for updates flowing from the conference itself.

 
PGO: Pacgold raises A$13 million to fund White Dam ramp up and North discovery follow up

Pacgold has received firm commitments for a two-tranche A$13 million placement, issuing approximately 86.67 million new shares at A$0.15 each. Tranche 1 (83,233,336 shares) settles 7 August 2026 under existing 7.1 and 7.1A capacity, with a smaller director tranche (3,433,334 shares) subject to AGM approval in late November 2026. Proceeds fund production ramp up and heap leach pad expansion at White Dam, plus follow up exploration at the White Dam North gold-copper discovery, including up to 35,000m of RC and 5,000m of diamond drilling. Near term catalysts include remaining White Dam North assays and a Vertigo MRE update.

 
PGO: Pacgold details placement following White Dam North discovery

Pacgold has lodged an Appendix 3B confirming issue of 86,666,670 new fully paid shares at AUD0.15 each, alongside 10,000,000 broker options exercisable at AUD0.225 and expiring 11 August 2029, issued to Euroz Hartleys and Taylor Collison as joint lead managers for a 5.5% cash fee. Shares are due for issue on 11 August 2026, split between the 7.1 (39,636,711 shares) and 7.1A (43,596,625 shares) placement capacities, with a further 3,433,334 shares to directors subject to AGM approval. Proceeds will accelerate exploration at White Dam, White Dam North and other sulphide targets, and fund expansion toward full scale gold production from existing oxide resources.

 
Quarterlies
TOK ($329m)   Tolu Minerals reported no substantive mining production during the quarter as Tolukuma remains in restart mode, holding approximately A$50 million cash after spending $9.74 million on exploration and evaluation, with 11,686m drilled across 68 holes in the June quarter (22,975m across 143 holes to 30 June 2026) delivering high-grade hits at the new Fundoot Splay (4.67m at 40.90g/t Au from 66m) and Gulbadi Splay (2m at 16.94g/t Au from 149m), underground development recommenced on the 1560 Level, with the updated MRE due late August 2026 and first gold production still targeted for Q1 2027 at a planned throughput of approximately 500 tonnes per day.
KZR ($47m)   Kalamazoo Resources closed the June quarter with A$2.1m cash as it advanced the Ashburton Gold Project, with a ~14,000m RC and diamond drilling program at Mt Olympus nearing completion (65 holes drilled, assays received for the first 16) returning nine intersections above 50 gram-metres including 21m @ 6.5g/t Au from 12m and 50m @ 2.6g/t Au from 129m in KARCD0141, and confirming mineralisation beyond the current resource envelope; the company appointed BurnVoir as financing adviser, brought on Andrew McDougall as CEO on 4 May 2026, and completed an oversubscribed A$8.0 million placement post quarter end (upsized from A$5.0 million), with an updated Mineral Resource Estimate targeted for Q4 2026 and the Mt Olympus PFS on track for H1 2027.
KZR ($47m)   Kalamazoo Resources ended the June quarter with $2.06 million cash (down from $8.8 million QoQ) after spending $5.83 million on exploration and evaluation (mostly the completed Mt Olympus Resource Drilling Program) and $851k in net operating outflows, with administration costs of $844k for the quarter; the company expects reduced exploration spend ahead and notes an $8 million placement (before costs) completed post-quarter, announced 20 July 2026, to fund ongoing operations.
ARI ($31m)   Arika Resources spent the June quarter advancing exploration rather than production, completing its first-phase 2026 drilling programme (around 100 holes for roughly 15,000m) at Yundamindra with results confirming mineralisation continuity over 2.5km of strike at Landed at Last, including 57m @ 1.42g/t Au from 125m (26AYRC024) and 13m @ 3.78g/t Au from 126m (26AYRC028); at Kookynie, surface sampling returned 82 samples above 1.0g/t Au and RC drilling commenced post-quarter at Cosmopolitan, Altona and Ithaca after Arika secured a $211,213 EIS co-funded grant; the company completed its transaction with Nex Metals for 100% ownership of both Yundamindra and Kookynie, and subsequent to quarter-end secured firm commitments for approximately $6.55 million via placement to fund an expanded exploration and resource growth programme of more than 20,000m of RC and diamond drilling across both projects.
LEX ($30m)   Lefroy Exploration progressed toward the Lucky Strike gold mine restart via profit-share partner BML Ventures, with dewatering pipeline works approved and grade control drilling and mining recommencement expected in the September quarter, while advancing Mt Martin (MRE now 9.1Mt @ 1.6g/t Au for 460koz, up 5% on 2024) with an initial 4,000m RC program and a Scoping Study, and Burns metallurgical test work returning up to 97.9% gold recovery; cash sat at $3.1 million as at 30 June 2026 after completing a $3.6 million placement during the quarter to fund Mt Martin and Burns work.
LEX ($30m)   Lefroy Exploration closed the June quarter with A$3.105 million cash (+A$1.681 million QoQ) after raising A$3.283 million in equity, spending A$959,000 on exploration and evaluation and A$406,000 in net operating cash outflow, against staff and administration costs of A$130,000 and A$289,000 respectively for the quarter, leaving an estimated 2.27 quarters of funding available.
LGM ($23m)   Legacy Minerals ended June with $6 million cash at Mt Carrington, a pre-development explorer with no production or unit costs to report this quarter, as it completed the eight-hole (1,841m) Mascotte diamond program (assays up to 9.7g/t Au and 40m @ 1.0g/t Au from 151m) and moved rigs onto an eight-hole, 4,000m program at the Emu Copper-Gold Prospect, while NSW approval was secured for a further eight-hole, 4,000m Mascotte Phase 2 program targeting resource growth next quarter.
LCL ($8m)   LCL Resources closed the June quarter debt-free with A$7.63 million cash (+A$4.58m QoQ) after receiving the final A$4.5 million Tiger Gold option payment for the completed Colombian asset sale and a A$150,000 Rio Tinto Stage 1 farm-in payment, while spending A$349k on PNG exploration, A$216k on admin/corporate costs, A$97k on related-party payments and A$31k on business development; the company retains a A$6.5 million deferred payment plus a 1% NSR on the divested Colombian assets and expects a further A$200,000 Rio Tinto milestone payment following the subsequent Kau Creek licence grant.

Day in Brief
 

Of the three confirmed announcements, two relate to Pacgold's A$13 million placement: firm commitments for approximately 86.67 million shares at A$0.15 each across two tranches, with Tranche 1 (83,233,336 shares) settling 7 August 2026 and a director tranche (3,433,334 shares) subject to AGM approval in late November 2026, followed by the Appendix 3B confirming issue of 86,666,670 shares plus 10,000,000 broker options to Euroz Hartleys and Taylor Collison, exercisable at AUD0.225 and expiring 11 August 2029, with shares due for issue on 11 August 2026. Proceeds fund production ramp up and heap leach pad expansion at White Dam, plus up to 35,000m of RC and 5,000m of diamond drilling at White Dam North. The third confirmed item is Ballard Mining's corporate presentation ahead of the Diggers & Dealers Mining Forum in Kalgoorlie from 3 to 5 August 2026, carrying its existing positioning but no new figures.
