OBM: Ora Banda sets out DRIVE to 300 growth plan and FY27 guidance at Diggers and Dealers

Ora Banda's Diggers and Dealers deck reiterates its aspirational ~300kozpa target and details FY27 guidance of 125,000 to 140,000oz gold at AISC of A$3,400/oz to A$3,600/oz, with total FY27 capital guidance of A$425 million. The company reports A$267.7 million cash at 30 June 2026, nil debt and a A$200 million undrawn revolving credit facility, giving pro forma liquidity of about A$468 million. HRN: Horizon returns broad high grade hits at Kingfisher, Hawk and Swan, Gum Creek

Final assays from RC drilling at Horizon Gold's 100% owned Gum Creek Project have returned high grade intercepts at three prospects. Kingfisher, which hosts open cut and underground resources of 78,900oz and 113,500oz respectively, returned 23m @ 5.26g/t Au from 188m (including 15m @ 7.65g/t Au from 192m, hole KFRC038) and 45m @ 1.24g/t Au from 114m (including 3m @ 6.79g/t Au from 114m, hole KFRC042). Hawk extended mineralisation down dip with 12m @ 2.30g/t Au from 140m (including 6m @ 4.26g/t Au from 140m, hole HKRC026), while Swan returned 16m @ 1.84g/t Au from 45m (including 7m @ 3.03g/t Au from 46m, hole SBRC150). Follow up RC and diamond drilling is planned at all three prospects ahead of resource updates later in 2026, with these additions sitting outside the recently completed DFS.

CAV: DFS-level metallurgical testwork confirms 98% to 99% gold recovery at Kookynie Gold Project

Carnavale Resources reports Definitive Feasibility Study level metallurgical testwork on the Kookynie Gold Project (60km south of Leonora, WA) has returned gold recoveries of 98% (oxide), 98% (intermediate) and 99% (fresh) at a 106-micron grind, using cyanidation leach testwork conducted with process water sourced from a Goldfields CIL operation. Gravity recoveries ranged 71% to 81%, cyanide consumption was low at 0.35kg/t to 0.44kg/t, and testwork found no preg-robbing, refractory mineralisation or deleterious elements across the oxide, transitional and fresh domains. Tailings characterisation is ongoing, and the company is now seeking a toll treatment or ore purchase agreement given the project relies on third-party processing.

Day in Brief
 

Diggers and Dealers dominated the session, with a run of investor decks from major and mid-tier gold producers restating rather than resetting the market's picture: Ramelius held its ~525kozpa FY30 target and A$5.8 billion market capitalisation, Northern Star detailed KCGM's 27Mtpa hub and 42Moz group resource base across two separate presentations, Evolution flagged higher Mungari discovery spend (A$33 million) alongside a margin-focused strategy citing an FY26 cash flow margin of A$1,958/oz, and Ora Banda set FY27 guidance of 125,000 to 140,000oz gold at AISC of A$3,400/oz to A$3,600/oz with A$267.7 million cash on hand. Black Cat Syndicate, Saturn Metals, Geopacific, Antipa, Minerals 260 (two releases), Benz Mining (two releases), Patronus Resources, Nexus Minerals and Golden Horse Minerals rounded out the forum-linked disclosures, several explicitly restating prior study metrics without new figures.
