BGD: Barton completes 39,000m Phase 2 resource upgrade drilling at Tunkillia

Barton Gold has finished Phase 2 resource upgrade drilling at Tunkillia, taking combined Phase 1 and Phase 2 drilling to 57,653m across 520 RC and diamond holes. The program targets upgrading the S1 and S2 optimised pit mineralisation to JORC Indicated, with the highest-value S1 material to Measured category. A Pre-Feasibility Study is underway with GR Engineering Services, targeting publication in the first quarter of CY2027.

 
GA8: GoldArc to acquire Niagara tenements, extending Leonora South

GoldArc has signed a binding agreement to acquire the Niagara West and North tenement package from Regener8 Resources, adjacent to its Leonora South project near Kookynie, WA. Upfront consideration is A$1.5 million in GoldArc shares at A$0.07 each, escrowed for six months, plus two contingent payments of A$500,000 each tied to reaching 100,000oz and 250,000oz JORC Mineral Resources at 1.5g/t Au or better. The ground sits along strike from GoldArc's Orion (26,409oz Au) and Sapphire (21,605oz Au) resources, with historical intercepts up to 70.52g/t Au from 7m. Completion requires shareholder approval, targeted via an EGM by 30 September 2026.

 
MPK: Many Peaks appoints new Chief Financial Officer

Many Peaks Minerals has appointed Jamie Byrde, a Chartered Accountant with over 20 years' resource sector finance and governance experience, as CFO effective 26 October 2026 on a 0.8 FTE basis with a base salary of A$264,000 per annum. He receives 500,000 options exercisable at A$1.00 and 500,000 performance rights tied to a feasibility study and financial close for the Ferké Gold Project in Côte d'Ivoire. The appointment comes as Many Peaks progresses its scoping study and drilling at Ferke.

 
GA8: Goldarc to issue shares for seven prospecting licences

Goldarc Resources plans to issue up to 39,610,389 shares to Regener8 as consideration for seven prospecting licences, comprising a 21,428,571-share upfront payment valued at A$1.5 million plus two milestone tranches of 9,090,909 shares each valued at A$500,000. Milestone shares price at the 5-day VWAP prior to vesting, with a floor of A$0.055 per share. The issue requires shareholder approval at a meeting expected on 30 September 2026, with securities to be issued on 2 October 2026, and upfront shares subject to six months voluntary escrow.

 
GBR: Great Boulder revamps MD and KMP pay structure

Following an independent remuneration review, the Board has lifted Managing Director Andrew Paterson's base salary to $414,000 plus superannuation, unchanged in three years. He is also eligible for an STI of up to $178,600 (40% of TFR) tied to 1.5Moz and 1.0Moz resource targets at Side Well and Peak Hill, and an LTI of up to $178,600 in performance rights linked to production start at Peak Hill and a mining lease grant at Mulga Bill. Performance rights require shareholder approval at the AGM in November 2026.

 
KCN: State Street Corporation becomes substantial holder in Kingsgate

State Street Corporation and its subsidiaries became a substantial holder in Kingsgate Consolidated on 3 August 2026, with a relevant interest in 13,316,701 ordinary shares representing 5.01% voting power. The holding is largely index and custodial in nature, spread across State Street Global Advisors entities and associated securities lending arrangements.

 
WAF: State Street reduces WAF stake below 6.5%

State Street Corporation and subsidiaries' voting power in West African Resources moved from 7.50% (85,763,431 shares) to 6.48% (74,067,174 shares) following changes effective 3 August 2026. The change is a passive, custodial-style movement across its managed and lending entities rather than a market transaction disclosed in the notice.

 
NWM: Norwest completes underwritten entitlement offer

Norwest Minerals has completed its non-renounceable entitlement offer, raising the full A$1.89 million after underwriters took up 164,042,640 shortfall securities. Entitlement and additional applications totalled 106,289,585 shares for A$744,342.41. Funds will go towards further exploration and development of the Bulgera Gold Project.

 
PDI: Citigroup drops below substantial holder threshold in PDI

Citigroup Global Markets Australia and related entities ceased to be a substantial holder in Predictive Discovery on 3 August 2026, a day after becoming one. The change reflects securities lending and ordinary market dealings, including a decrease of 102,607,125 shares held by Citigroup Global Markets Australia. No operational implications for the company.

 
BNZ: Benz Mining routine CDI count for July 2026

Benz Mining reports 291,933,473 CDIs on issue at end of July 2026, up 64,031 from the prior month via net transfers of ordinary shares into CDI form. Unquoted ordinary shares fell to 46,961,590. Options and performance rights on issue were unchanged.

 
EVN: Evolution sets date for FY26 results briefing

Evolution will lodge its FY26 Financial Report and Appendix 4E pre-opening on 19 August 2026. Managing Director Lawrie Conway and CFO Fran Summerhayes will host a conference call at 10.30am Sydney time that day, with a listen-only audio stream and analyst Q&A dial-in available. FY26 production was 715koz of gold and 66kt of copper, with Group operating mine cash flow of $3,394 million at an All-in Sustaining Cost of $1,717 per ounce.

 
STN: Saturn Metals completes placement with compliance notice

Saturn Metals has issued 63,783,443 fully paid ordinary shares at A$0.40 per share under the placement announced on 29 July 2026. The notice, lodged under section 708A(5)(e) of the Corporations Act, confirms the shares were issued without a disclosure document and that the company has complied with its Chapter 2M and continuous disclosure obligations.
