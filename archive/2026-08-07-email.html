AUE: Aurum extends high-grade gold at BMT3, Boundiali

Aurum Resources has returned assays from 36 diamond holes (9,452m) across BMT3, BMT2 and BMT1 at its 3.22Moz Boundiali Gold Project in Côte d'Ivoire. The standout intercept was 1.76m @ 150.87g/t Au from 485.94m, including 0.72m @ 367.05g/t Au, at BMT3, with mineralisation extending outside the current resource. Results will feed into the next Boundiali resource update, targeted for the end of Q3 CY2026, ahead of a DFS due late CY2026.

 
MRT: Maritana starts drilling at Wilsons Gold, 400m from Black Swan hub

Maritana Minerals has commenced a 4,000 metre aircore drilling program at the Wilsons Gold Prospect, located approximately 400m from its Black Swan Processing Hub. The program is the first drilling to test gold potential beneath a 2.5km by 1.5km gold-in-soil anomaly, where past rock chips graded up to 1.25g/t Au and historical drilling returned 0.4m @ 6.41g/t Au. Assay results are expected in late September or early October 2026.

 
ORE: Monthly CDI count edges down as holders shift to TSX common stock

Orezone's routine Appendix 4A shows CDIs on issue at 43,352,323 as at end July 2026, down 834,900 from 44,187,223 the prior month, reflecting net transfers between CDIs and TSX-quoted common stock. Common stock on issue rose to 624,750,667. Options over 400,000 shares expiring 16 July 2026 were exercised during the period.
