GA8: GoldArc hits best Mt Stirling Well intercept yet in fifth grade control batch

GoldArc's fifth grade control batch at Mt Stirling Well (M37/1305) returned the deposit's strongest intercept to date of 2m @ 69.7g/t Au from 23m, including 1m @ 137.0g/t Au (26BSWRC906), from 85 holes totalling approximately 2,245m. Five consecutive batches have now returned high-grade results, feeding directly into mining models. The program, fully funded by BML Ventures under a 50/50 net profit share, has completed approximately 27,537m combined, with a 6,600m RC program set to start around Mt Stirling in August 2026.

 
CZN: Corazon appoints Rob Curtis as Non-Executive Chair

Corazon Mining has appointed Rob Curtis, formerly of Oxiana, OZ Minerals and EMR Capital, as Non-Executive Chair, replacing Kristie Young who has resigned from the board. Curtis will receive director's fees of $75,000 per annum plus superannuation, and is proposed to receive zero exercise price options subject to shareholder approval. The appointment comes as Corazon advances its Chalice Gold Project, which hosts a resource of 191,000oz at 2.7g/t Au, alongside the Two Pools and Feather Cap projects in Western Australia.

 
LGM: New IP survey defines untested gold-silver target at Mt Carrington

Ground IP surveys at the Mascotte Prospect, Mt Carrington, have defined multiple large, untested chargeability anomalies exceeding 30mV/V, sitting outside the current 1.6Moz AuEq Mineral Resource. The zones lie along a 300m strike, open to the northeast and southwest, adjacent to prior drilling including 40m @ 1.0g/t Au from 151m (MSDD001). Drilling is underway to test the targets.

 
AS1: Capital DI reduces Asara holding to 9.59%

Capital DI Limited's voting power in Asara Resources fell from 11.46% to 9.59% after selling 36,000,000 shares on market on 6 August 2026 for A$4,041,900. This followed earlier additions via a placement and an on-market purchase in June and July 2026. Capital DI now holds 200,000,000 ordinary shares.

 
TRE: Eagle Eye group's stake diluted to 31.82% in Toubani

Eagle Eye Asset Holdings and associated Gagan Gupta entities now hold 341,223,281 shares in Toubani Resources, with voting power easing to 31.82% from 34.66%. The change follows a A$23,973,532.50 subscription for 79,911,775 shares under the institutional component of the entitlement offer, taken up in full pro rata.

 
PGO: Pacgold issues Tranche 1 shares from A$13 million placement

Pacgold has issued 83,233,336 fully paid ordinary shares under Tranche 1 of the A$13 million placement announced on 31 July 2026. The cleansing notice confirms compliance with Chapter 2M and sections 674 and 674A of the Corporations Act, allowing the shares to trade freely.

 
VAL: Franklin Resources emerges as substantial holder in Valiant Gold

Franklin Resources, Inc. and its affiliates became a substantial holder in Valiant Gold on 7 August 2026, holding 27,403,800 ordinary shares representing 5.07% voting power. The stake was built through a series of on-market acquisitions between 4 May 2026 and 7 August 2026, including 3,060,000 shares bought on 7 August 2026 for A$672,966.
