AUE: Aurum extends gold at Boundiali's BST1 and BST2

Aurum Resources reported assays from 46 diamond holes (8,835.14m) at Boundiali's BST tenement, including 3m @ 80.09g/t Au from 66m (BSDD0091) at the 22Mt @ 0.8g/t Au BST1 deposit and 3m @ 27.65g/t Au from 144m (BSDD0077) at the BST2 prospect, which sits outside the current resource. Results feed into the next Boundiali MRE update targeted for end Q3 CY2026, ahead of a DFS expected late CY2026. Aurum holds A$54.7 million cash (30 June 2026, unaudited) and is running 16 owned diamond rigs across a 130,000m CY2026 drilling program.

 
MEK
Meeka completes Mt Holland South gold deposits acquisition

Meeka has completed acquisition of the Blue Vein, Bushpig and Razorback gold deposits near Mt Holland, WA, covering 71km2 of tenure over ~24km of gold-hosting BIF units in a district that produced ~1.2Moz @ 5.12g/t Au historically. Consideration comprises A$10 million cash at completion, A$10 million deferred to 13 November 2026, and 117,804,881 shares (3.8% of enlarged capital). JORC 2012 resources for the three deposits are due in October 2026, with drilling at Blue Vein to start January 2027.

Meeka issues shares as consideration for Mt Holland South

Meeka Metals issued 117,804,881 fully paid ordinary shares as part consideration for the acquisition of the Mt Holland South project. The issue is covered by a section 708A(5)(e) cleansing notice, with an Appendix 2A lodged the same day. No new operational or financial detail on the project was provided.

 
NVO: Novo's H1 2026 MD&A details Belltopper target and cash build

Novo's interim MD&A for the six months to 30 June 2026 shows cash and short-term investments of A$9,579,000, up from A$7,741,000 at December 2025, after two placement tranches raised approximately A$5,900,000 and A$1,930,000. The Belltopper Exploration Target stands at 2.1Mt to 3.1Mt at 6.7g/t Au to 8.9g/t Au for 460koz to 880koz Au, with a 2,500m diamond drilling program planned for Q4 2026. Maiden RC drilling was completed at Wyloo SE and Cronus in the Pilbara, and a 2700m RC program is planned at Tibooburra in H2 2026.

 
RXL: High-grade drill results and construction progress at Youanmi

Underground diamond drilling at United North has returned further high-grade intercepts, including 2.24m @ 39.33g/t Au from 220.70m (UNN26_014) and 5.00m @ 6.39g/t Au from 108.00m (UNN26_011). Ore drive development is now extending beyond the DFS mine plan, with July mining advance of 522m exceeding DFS rates. Rox has also signed a Power Purchase Agreement with Pacific Energy for a thermal, solar and battery facility, with first gold still targeted for mid-CY2027.

 
AAR: Theia Deeps drilling extends high-grade mineralisation below resource

New diamond drill assays from AMRCD262, AMRCD376 and AMRCD378 at the Mandilla Gold Project's Theia deposit have returned broad zones of mineralisation below the April 2026 resource shell, including 52.75m at 4.67g/t Au from 445.25m and 45m at 1.39g/t Au from 389m. The deep diamond program has been expanded from 6 to 21 holes (11,000m), with 13 holes now completed and results pending for the remainder.

 
GA8: Final grade control round confirms high-grade shoots at Mt Stirling Well

GoldArc's sixth and final grade control round at Mt Stirling Well (M37/1305) returned 1m @ 59.9g/t Au from 24m (26BSWRC973), with seven further intercepts above 10g/t Au. Six consecutive rounds have now returned high-grade gold well above the deposit's 2.3g/t Au resource grade (198,000t for 15,000oz), with grade control coverage now complete and feeding into Mt Stirling mine planning. A ~6,600m RC program testing soil gold anomalies around the Mt Stirling deposits is set to start in August 2026.

 
GG8: Val discovery grows further at Vivien, next to permitted mine

Drilling has extended the high-grade Val discovery at Gorilla's 100%-owned Vivien Gold Project to over 400m of strike and 400m down-dip across multiple lodes, remaining open in all directions. Best results include 0.9m @ 23.0g/t Au from 311.3m and 4.5m @ 4.0g/t Au from 321.9m in VIVEX025, with a new footwall position identified in VIVEX020 alongside a DHEM conductor. Val sits 30m from the Vivien pit wall and 70m from underground infrastructure, with drilling set to recommence in coming weeks.

 
GSN: Deep extensions confirmed at Mon Ami with high-grade RC results

Assays from the first six of 16 RC holes at the 100%-owned Mon Ami Gold Project near Laverton have extended known mineralisation up to 70m down dip and 55m down plunge. The standout intercept was 16m at 3.37g/t Au from 178m, including 4m at 8.54g/t Au, in hole 26MARC001. Mon Ami hosts a JORC resource of 1.56Mt at 1.11g/t Au for 55.5Koz gold on a granted mining licence. Assays are pending for a further 10 holes at the Blanc Platt target, expected within 1-2 weeks.

 
KZR: Kalamazoo appoints Teresa Pacey as Chief Financial Officer

Kalamazoo has appointed Teresa Pacey as Chief Financial Officer effective 28 September 2026. Ms Pacey is a Fellow CPA with more than 17 years' finance experience, most recently as Group Financial Controller and Head of Finance at CoMade Group. The appointment adds financial and commercial capability as Kalamazoo progresses the 1.44Moz Ashburton Gold Project through its Pre-Feasibility Study.

 
AUC: Ausgold halted pending change of control announcement

Ausgold requested a trading halt pending an announcement on a potential change of control transaction. The halt remains until the earlier of normal trading on 19 August 2026 or release of the announcement, which the company expects to make before trading resumes on that date.

Gold explorers supplied most of the day's news. Aurum Resources extended high-grade mineralisation at both BST1 and BST2 at Boundiali, feeding into a Q3 CY2026 resource update and a DFS due late CY2026, with A$54.7 million cash on hand at 30 June 2026.
