EVN
Evolution posts record profit, cash flow and dividend in FY26

Evolution Mining reported statutory net profit after tax of A$1,475.1 million for FY26, up 59% on FY25, with underlying NPAT of A$1,562.9 million. Revenue rose 28% to A$5,558.8 million and underlying EBITDA hit a record A$3,171.3 million. The group produced 715koz gold and 66kt copper at AISC of A$1,717/oz, moved to a net cash position with A$1,347.5 million cash, and declared a record final dividend of 21.0 cents per share, taking FY26 total dividends to 41.0 cents per share.

Evolution posts record FY26 profit, lifts dividend payout policy

Evolution reported record underlying net profit of A$1,563 million, up 63%, and record underlying EBITDA of A$3,171 million at a 57% margin. Group cash flow rose 76% to A$1,389 million, moving the balance sheet to net cash of A$19 million. The Board lifted its dividend payout policy to 60% of Group cash flow and declared a record fully franked final dividend of 21.0cps, taking FY26 dividends to 41.0cps (A$833 million). FY27 guidance targets 660,000oz to 730,000oz gold and 63,000t to 70,000t copper at AISC of A$1,795 to A$1,995 per ounce.

Evolution reports FY26 climate disclosures under AASB S2

Evolution's first AASB S2-aligned Climate Report shows FY26 Scope 1 and 2 emissions of 741,336tCO2-e, a ~19% reduction against its FY20 baseline, progressing toward its target of a 30% cut by 2030 and net zero by 2050. The report notes the previously flagged Carnaby Resources acquisition, worth approximately $213 million, with a scheme meeting expected late October to early November 2026.

Evolution announces Board changes

Non-Executive Director Tommy McKeith will retire from the Evolution Board at the conclusion of the AGM in November 2026, having joined in February 2014 and chaired the Nomination and Remuneration Committee. Geoscientist John Vann, formerly of Anglo American, AngloGold Ashanti and SRK Consulting, will join the Board on 1 December 2026 on the Risk and Sustainability Committee. Vicky Binns becomes Nomination and Remuneration Committee Chair and Fiona Hick joins that committee, both effective 1 September 2026.

 
PC2: Spring Hill metallurgy shows high gold extraction at coarse grind

PFS testwork at Spring Hill returned gold extraction of 90.2% to 99.4% across weathered, transitional and fresh domains, with weathered zones free-milling to 99.4% at a coarse 150µm grind. Gravity recovery ranged 33.1% to 48.9%, with low lime and cyanide consumption. True specific gravity results (2.87 to 3.07t/m3) were higher than the 2.57 to 2.77t/m3 used in the July Interim MRE, with further density testwork to feed into an updated MRE targeted for mid December 2026. PFS remains on track for mid-November 2026.

 
KAL: KalGold executes expanded heritage deal covering Pinjin and Bulong Taurus

Kalgoorlie Gold Mining has signed an updated Heritage Protection Agreement with the Kakarra Aboriginal Corporation, replacing an earlier deal limited to tenement E28/3134 following the Federal Court's native title determination for the Kakarra people. The agreement now covers all of KalGold's tenements and its Bulong Taurus gold rights, and is expected to speed heritage surveys, drill clearances and new licence grants at Pinjin and Bulong Taurus. Next catalyst is progress on forthcoming exploration and resource drill programs at Pinjin.

 
GHM: New high-grade zone confirmed at depth, Hopes Hill

Golden Horse has returned 16.6m @ 3.4g/t Au from 392.4m in hole 26HHDD031, including 5.0m @ 9.9g/t Au from 399.0m, part of an emerging high-grade zone beneath the northern end of the historic Hopes Hill pit. Further south, 26HHRC162 returned 21m @ 1.5g/t Au from 215m including 13m @ 2.1g/t Au from 221m, extending the southern plunging shoot. Two diamond rigs and an RC rig remain active ahead of a planned maiden Mineral Resource Estimate.

 
AS1: Asara sells non-core Paguanta project to focus on Kada

Asara has signed a binding agreement to sell its 74.79% interest in the Paguanta copper and silver-lead-zinc project in Chile to Ajax Resources PLC. Total consideration is US$1.4 million, comprising US$50,000 cash and US$350,000 in Ajax shares on completion, plus two US$500,000 milestone payments tied to future Ore Reserve announcements. The project has been on care and maintenance since 2018, and the divestment lets Asara focus on its flagship Kada Gold Project in Guinea. Conditions precedent must be satisfied within 180 days of signing.

 
MM8: Medallion Metals halted pending capital raising

Medallion Metals shares are in a trading halt pending an announcement on a capital raising. The halt is expected to lift with commencement of normal trading on Friday, 21 August 2026, or on earlier release of the raising announcement. No raise size or price has yet been disclosed.

 
RXL: Rox signs Native Title Mining Agreement for Youanmi

Rox has executed a binding Native Title Mining Agreement with the Badimia Barna Native Title Claim Applicant covering the Youanmi Gold Mine project area. It secures consent for grant and renewal of all future project tenure and mining approvals, and sets a framework for heritage protection, employment, training and contracting, backed by an upfront payment plus a production-based royalty. The deal marks a key step as Rox targets first gold from Youanmi in mid-CY2027.
