PGL: More high-grade gold hits extend Mt York at depth

Pilbara Gold's latest drilling at the 2.1Moz Mt York Project has extended gold mineralisation at Main Hill and Main Hill Extension, with best results including 10m @ 4.02g/t Au from 262m (26MYRCD004) and 22m @ 2.04g/t Au from 122m (26MYDD024A). Mineralisation remains open at depth and is thickening at Main Hill Extension. The 60,000m program (increased from 50,000m) is 28,635m complete, with an updated Mineral Resource Estimate due early 2027 and a PFS in mid-2027.

 
BNZ: Benz raises A$150 million to fast-track Glenburgh resource

Benz Mining has secured firm commitments for a A$150 million institutional placement of 40.4 million CDIs at A$3.71 each, a nil discount to last close. Proceeds will add six rigs to lift the Glenburgh fleet to 14 rigs across 20 shifts, accelerating the planned 450,000m drill program. The company is targeting a maiden Mineral Resource Estimate for Glenburgh in H1 2027, with New CDIs settling 28 August 2026.

 
PDI: Predictive Discovery becomes PDI Gold, shares to consolidate 5-into-1

Shareholders approved the company's rebrand from Predictive Discovery Limited to PDI Gold Limited, effective on ASX from 9 September 2026, with the ticker remaining PDI on both ASX and TSX. A 5-into-1 share consolidation was also approved, effective 24 August 2026, with normal settlement trading on a consolidated basis to commence 7 September 2026.

 
MAT: Matsa halts trading ahead of Devon Pit Gold Project update

Matsa Resources requested a trading halt pending an announcement on operations at its Devon Pit Gold Project. The halt applies until trading resumes on Wednesday 26 August 2026, or earlier if the announcement is released first. No detail on the nature of the update was given.

 
NWM: Norwest pushes back Bulgera heap leach Scoping Study

Norwest Minerals will delay release of the Bulgera Gold Heap Leach Project Scoping Study until complete results from the 70-day column leach and fresh rock IBRT metallurgical programs are incorporated, now expected in early October 2026. The column leach testwork on coarse-crushed oxide material is tracking in line with initial bottle-roll recoveries. Results will feed into processing and financial models via KCAA ahead of the study's release.
