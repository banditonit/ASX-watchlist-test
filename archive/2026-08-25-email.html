RMS
Ramelius lifts Ore Reserves 79% and Mineral Resources 17%

Ramelius' 30 June 2026 statement shows Mineral Resources up 17% to 260Mt at 1.7g/t Au for 14Moz, and Ore Reserves up 79% to 70Mt at 1.9g/t Au for 4.3Moz. Growth was driven by maiden Ore Reserves at Never Never (6.8Mt at 7.4g/t Au for 1.6Moz) and Roe (4.4Mt at 1.8g/t Au for 260koz), plus FY26 drilling that added 1.8Moz Au at 1.5g/t Au at a discovery cost of about A$55/oz. An updated FY27 to FY30 production and cost outlook is due in September.

Ramelius posts record FY26 earnings and reserve growth

Ramelius reported FY26 underlying EBITDA of A$765.4 million and underlying NPAT of A$319.9 million, with cash and gold on hand of A$649.6 million. Ore Reserves grew 79% year on year to 4.3Moz at 1.9g/t and Mineral Resources rose 17% to 14.0Moz at 1.7g/t. FY27 guidance and a four-year outlook are due in September 2026.

 
HRN: High-grade hits extend Heron South, Howards and Melbourne Bitter

RC drilling at Gum Creek returned 6m @ 12.67g/t Au from 114m including 1m @ 58.70g/t Au at Heron South, alongside 6m @ 5.57g/t Au from 70m at Howards and 21m @ 2.14g/t Au from 24m at Melbourne Bitter. Howards drilling confirmed an 800m southern extension to mineralisation, while Heron South and Melbourne Bitter results point to potential resource growth down dip and along strike. Follow-up RC and diamond drilling is planned at all three prospects ahead of updated resource estimates later in 2026.

 
BTR: Visible gold widens Two Mile Hill tonalite to 500m thick

Two deep diamond holes at Two Mile Hill, Sandstone, intersected 400m to 500m of mineralised tonalite with 53 logged visible gold occurrences, both ending in mineralisation and extending the host unit to over 300m horizontal width and open below 700m. The deposit already hosts a 731koz @ 1.6g/t Au resource. Two more holes remain in the 3,400m program, with assays expected late September 2026.

 
CZN: Corazon defines maiden Exploration Target at Two Pools gold project

Corazon Mining has released a maiden Exploration Target for its 100%-owned Two Pools project in Western Australia's Gascoyne region, of 1.4Mt to 2.15Mt at 1.6g/t to 1.9g/t Au for 72,000oz to 132,000oz of contained gold across Northern, Central and Southern target areas. The Northern Target is best drilled, spanning approximately 540m east-west, 350m north-south and 190m vertically from about 92 drillholes, while Central and Southern remain wider-spaced at up to 200m and open. Corazon plans approximately 5,000m of RC drilling across all three areas over the next six months to test the target and support future resource estimation.

 
WGX: Westgold outlines 2.9Mtpa Meekatharra expansion plan

A Scoping Study proposes lifting Meekatharra processing capacity from 1.8Mtpa to 2.9Mtpa by FY28, adding about 47kozpa for total production of 1.6Moz over 10 years. Indicative capital is up to A$100 million with a 9 month payback and NPV of approximately A$1.9 billion at A$5,500/oz gold. Feasibility work is now underway ahead of a potential investment decision in late FY27.

 
DTM
Dart Mining defines two priority drill targets at Walwa tin project

Dart Mining has compiled and verified 150 historic drill holes (7,672m) at its Walwa tin-tantalum project in Victoria, identifying two priority targets: a deep greisen-hosted tin zone at 400m to 600m depth, and repeating mineralised dykes indicated by seismic surveys below known workings. Historic pre-JORC estimates include 8.64Mt @ 0.163% Sn and a higher-grade zone of 993,000t @ 0.38% Sn. Diamond drilling to test these targets is expected to start in October 2026.

Dart Mining details consideration options for Yamarna project deal

Dart Mining has lodged an Appendix 3B for 18,823,532 unlisted options exercisable at A$0.0110 and expiring 30 September 2029, issued as consideration for the Yamarna project acquisition announced on 20 August 2026. The options require shareholder approval, expected at a meeting on 30 September 2026, ahead of a proposed issue date of 5 October 2026.

 
YRL: New high-grade domain confirmed at Arrakis

Further infill RC and diamond results from the Arrakis gold discovery in Yandal's Ironstone Well-Barwidgee project have defined a third high-grade domain within the 1.3km mineralised system. Best results include 17.5m @ 2.3g/t Au from 127.5m and 17.0m @ 1.1g/t Au from 162.0m in 26IWBRC0121D, located about 50m down-dip of the previously reported 6m @ 28.3g/t Au hit. The domain remains untested down-plunge below the Yueh Shear Zone, with two further diamond tail results due early September.

 
PC2: PC Gold halted ahead of capital raising news

PC Gold requested a trading halt pending an announcement regarding a capital raising. The halt will remain in place until the earlier of normal trading resuming on Thursday, 27 August 2026, or release of the capital raising announcement. No details of the raise size or price have yet been disclosed.
