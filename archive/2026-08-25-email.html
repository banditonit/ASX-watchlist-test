RMS
Ramelius lifts Ore Reserves 79% and Mineral Resources 17%

Ramelius' 2026 Resources and Reserves Statement shows Mineral Resources up 17% to 260Mt at 1.7g/t Au for 14Moz, and Ore Reserves up 79% to 70Mt at 1.9g/t Au for 4.3Moz. Growth was driven by maiden Ore Reserves at Never Never (6.8Mt at 7.4g/t Au for 1.6Moz) and Roe (4.4Mt at 1.8g/t Au for 260koz), plus FY26 drilling that added 1.8Moz Au at 1.5g/t Au at a discovery cost of ~A$55/oz. An updated production and cost outlook to FY30 is due in September 2026.

Ramelius lifts Ore Reserves 79% and Resources 17% in FY26 update

Ramelius' FY26 investor presentation confirms Mineral Resources of 14.0Moz at 1.7g/t (up 17% year-on-year after depletion) and Ore Reserves of 4.3Moz at 1.9g/t (up 79%). FY26 underlying EBITDA reached A$765.4 million with underlying NPAT of A$319.9 million, and cash and gold on hand stood at A$649.6 million. FY27 exploration targets 850,000oz to 1,600,000oz at 1.9g/t to 2.4g/t Au, with FY27 guidance due in September 2026.

 
HRN: High-grade RC results extend Heron South, Howards and Melbourne Bitter

Final assay results from RC drilling at Gum Creek's Howards, Heron South and Melbourne Bitter prospects have confirmed an 800m southern extension to mineralisation at Howards, with Heron South returning 6m @ 12.67g/t Au from 114m including 1m @ 58.70g/t Au from 114m. Melbourne Bitter drilling points to resource expansion potential south of the existing 0.48Mt @ 1.39g/t Au resource. Follow-up RC and diamond drilling is planned at all three prospects ahead of updated resource estimates later in 2026.

 
BTR: Visible gold logged through 500m tonalite at Two Mile Hill

Two deep diamond holes at the Sandstone Project's Two Mile Hill deposit intersected 400m to 500m of mineralised tonalite, with 53 visible gold occurrences logged, mostly in quartz veins with galena and pyrite. Both holes ended in mineralised tonalite, extended beyond planned depth, with the unit now interpreted at over 300m horizontal thickness and open below 700m. Two Mile Hill-Shillington currently hosts 731koz @ 1.6g/t Au; assays are due late September 2026, with two more holes remaining in the ~3,400m program.

 
CZN: Corazon defines maiden gold Exploration Target at Two Pools

Corazon Mining has defined a maiden Exploration Target of 1.4Mt to 2.15Mt at 1.6g/t to 1.9g/t Au for approximately 72,000oz to 132,000oz of contained gold at its 100% owned Two Pools Gold Project in Western Australia. The Northern Target is most advanced, defined by around 92 drillholes, while Central and Southern areas remain under-drilled with spacing up to 200m. A further ~5,000m RC drilling program is planned over the next six months to test the target and support future Mineral Resource estimation.

 
WGX: Westgold outlines brownfields expansion of Meekatharra processing hub

Westgold's scoping study lifts Meekatharra throughput from 1.8Mtpa to 2.9Mtpa (a 61% increase), adding about 47kozpa for total production of 1.6Moz over 10 years. Indicative capital is A$65 million to A$100 million with a 9-month payback, and project NPV improves by about A$1.1 billion at A$5,500/oz gold. Feasibility work is underway ahead of a potential investment decision in late FY27.

 
DTM
Dart Mining defines two priority drill targets at Walwa tin project

Dart Mining has compiled and verified 150 historic drill holes (7,672m) at its Walwa tin-tantalum project in Victoria, identifying two high-priority targets: a larger high-grade greisen zone 400m to 600m below surface, and repeating mineralised dykes indicated by seismic survey below known workings. Historic pre-JORC estimates include 8.64Mt @ 0.163% Sn and a higher-grade zone of 993,000t @ 0.38% Sn. Diamond drilling of the targets is expected to start in October 2026.

Dart Mining details consideration options for Yamarna deal

Dart Mining plans to issue 18,823,532 options exercisable at A$0.011, expiring 30 September 2029, as consideration for the previously announced Yamarna gold project acquisition. The issue requires shareholder approval at a meeting expected on 30 September 2026, with securities proposed to be issued on 5 October 2026.

 
YRL: New high-grade domain confirmed at Arrakis gold discovery

Yandal Resources has returned further infill RC and diamond results from the Arrakis discovery within its Ironstone Well-Barwidgee project, including 17.5m @ 2.3g/t Au from 127.5m and 17.0m @ 1.1g/t Au from 162.0m in 26IWBRC0121D, located about 50m down-dip of the earlier 6m @ 28.3g/t Au hit reported on 10 August 2026. The results define a third high-grade domain within the 1.3km Arrakis system, with the down-plunge extension below the Yueh Shear Zone remaining untested. Two further diamond tail results are expected in early September 2026.

 
PC2: PC Gold halted ahead of capital raising news

PC Gold has entered a trading halt at its own request pending an announcement on a capital raising. The halt will lift on the earlier of normal trading resuming on 27 August 2026 or the raising announcement being released.
