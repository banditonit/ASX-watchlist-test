RMS
Ramelius lifts Ore Reserves 79% and Mineral Resources 17%

Ramelius' 30 June 2026 update shows Mineral Resources up 17% to 260Mt at 1.7g/t Au for 14Moz, and Ore Reserves up 79% to 70Mt at 1.9g/t Au for 4.3Moz. Growth was driven by maiden reserves at Never Never (6.8Mt at 7.4g/t Au for 1.6Moz) and Roe underground (4.4Mt at 1.8g/t Au for 260koz), plus Galaxy drilling. FY26 exploration added 1.8Moz at a discovery cost of about A$55/oz. Updated FY27 to FY30 production and cost guidance is due in September.

Ramelius lifts Ore Reserves 79% to 4.3Moz after FY26 update

Ramelius' FY26 Resources and Reserves update shows Mineral Resources up 17% year-on-year to 14.0Moz at 1.7g/t Au and Ore Reserves up 79% to 4.3Moz at 1.9g/t Au, both after mining depletion. Underlying EBITDA came in at A$765.4 million with underlying NPAT of A$319.9 million, and FY26 exploration discovered 1.8Moz at 1.5g/t Au at a discovery cost of about A$55/oz. FY27 exploration targets 850 to 1,600koz at 1.9 to 2.4g/t Au, with FY27 guidance due in September.

First Sentier emerges as substantial holder in Ramelius

First Sentier Group Limited became a substantial holder in Ramelius Resources on 20 August 2026, with a relevant interest in 94,974,598 shares representing 5.03% voting power. The interest is held across its investment manager subsidiaries First Sentier Investors RQI Pty Ltd and First Sentier Investors (Australia) IM Ltd, plus a stake attributed to ultimate parent Mitsubishi UFJ Financial Group.

 
HRN
Horizon extends mineralisation at Howards, Heron South, Melbourne Bitter

RC drilling across three Gum Creek prospects has returned a string of gold intercepts, headlined by 6m @ 12.67g/t Au from 114m including 1m @ 58.70g/t Au at Heron South. Howards drilling confirmed an 800m southern extension to known mineralisation, while Melbourne Bitter results point to resource growth to the south. Follow-up RC and diamond drilling is planned at all three prospects ahead of updated resource estimates later in 2026.

L1 Capital increases substantial holding in Horizon Gold

L1 Capital Pty Ltd has increased its voting power in Horizon Gold from 5.30% to 6.59%, now holding 12,840,403 shares. The change follows a series of on-market purchases between June and August 2026, including 985,801 shares bought on 20 August 2026 for A$986,659.01.

 
BTR: Visible gold logged through 500m tonalite intercept at Two Mile Hill

Two diamond holes into the Two Mile Hill deposit at Sandstone each intersected 400m to 500m of mineralised tonalite, with 53 individual visible gold occurrences logged across both holes, mostly in quartz veins with galena and pyrite. The tonalite is now interpreted at over 300m horizontal width and open below 700m, extending beyond the existing 731koz @ 1.6g/t Au Two Mile Hill-Shillington resource envelope. Assays are pending, expected late September 2026, with two more holes remaining in the 3,400m program.

 
CZN: Maiden Exploration Target defined at Two Pools gold project

Corazon has defined a maiden Exploration Target of 1.4Mt to 2.15Mt at 1.6g/t to 1.9g/t Au for approximately 72,000oz to 132,000oz contained gold across the Northern, Central and Southern target areas at its 100%-owned Two Pools project in Western Australia. The Northern target is the most advanced, based on approximately 92 drillholes, while Central and Southern areas remain under-drilled at spacing up to 200m. A ~5,000m RC drilling program is planned over the next six months to test the target and support future resource estimation.

 
WGX: Westgold outlines A$100M Meekatharra brownfields expansion

Westgold's scoping study lifts Meekatharra processing capacity from 1.8Mtpa to 2.9Mtpa, adding about 47kozpa once commissioned in FY28 for total production of 1.6Moz over 10 years. Indicative capital is A$65 million to A$100 million with a 9-month payback, and project NPV improves by about A$1.1 billion at A$5,500/oz gold. Feasibility work now proceeds toward a potential investment decision in late FY27.

 
DTM
Dart Mining defines two priority drill targets at Walwa tin project

Dart Mining has compiled and verified 150 historic drill holes (7,672m) at its Walwa tin-tantalum project in Victoria, generating two high-priority targets: a larger tonnage greisen-hosted tin zone at 400m to 600m depth, and repeating mineralised dykes indicated by seismic below known workings. Historic pre-JORC estimates include 8.64Mt @ 0.163% Sn and a higher-grade zone of 993,000t @ 0.38% Sn. Diamond drilling of both targets is expected to start in October 2026.

Dart Mining sets terms for Yamarna acquisition options

Dart Mining lodged an Appendix 3B for 18,823,532 options exercisable at A$0.011 and expiring 30 September 2029, issued as consideration for the Yamarna project acquisition announced on 20 August 2026. The issue requires shareholder approval at a meeting expected 30 September 2026, with securities to be issued 5 October 2026.

 
YRL: New high-grade domain identified at Arrakis gold discovery

Yandal Resources reports further infill RC and diamond assays from Arrakis, within the Ironstone Well-Barwidgee project, defining a third high-grade domain roughly 50m down-dip of the 6m @ 28.3g/t Au hit reported on 10 August 2026. Best results include 17.5m @ 2.3g/t Au from 127.5m and 17.0m @ 1.1g/t Au from 162.0m in 26IWBRC0121D, plus 10m @ 1.8g/t Au from 42m in 26IWBRC0129. Interpretations suggest a south-plunging zone 100m to 150m wide, still untested below the Yueh Shear Zone, with further diamond tail results due early September 2026.

 
LEX: Substantial holder notice filed blank

LEX lodged a Form 603 notice of initial substantial holder, but the template fields for the holder's name, voting power and relevant interest were not filled in. No figures on the stake size or acquisition price are disclosed in the document provided.

 
AAR: Colin Petroulas emerges as substantial holder in Astral Resources

Colin Petroulas became a substantial holder on 24 August 2026, holding 93,200,000 ordinary shares representing 5.175% voting power. The notice discloses no acquisition consideration or associate details.

 
AUC: L1 Capital increases substantial holding in Ausgold

L1 Capital Pty Ltd's voting power in Ausgold rose from 7.48% to 9.11%, after on-market purchases including 6,679,230 shares on 20 August 2026 for A$9,166,895.52 and 503,574 shares on 24 August 2026 for A$707,253.65. L1 now holds 50,058,795 shares. The buying follows OceanaGold's agreed acquisition of Ausgold announced 17 August 2026.

 
GMD
JPMorgan becomes substantial holder in Genesis Minerals

JPMorgan Chase & Co. and its affiliates became a substantial holder in Genesis Minerals on 20 August 2026, with a relevant interest in 85,704,704 ordinary shares representing 7.30% voting power. The holding includes securities lending and custodial positions across several JPMorgan entities. The notice follows JPMorgan ceasing to be a substantial holder the previous day.

First Sentier emerges as substantial holder in Genesis Minerals

First Sentier Group and related entities became a substantial holder in Genesis Minerals on 20 August 2026, holding 66,344,066 fully paid ordinary shares representing 5.65% voting power. The interest comprises 28,648,994 shares held by First Sentier entities and 37,695,072 shares in which parent Mitsubishi UFJ Financial Group has voting power.

 
PNR: State Street cuts Pantoro stake below 6%

State Street Corporation and its subsidiaries' voting power in Pantoro Gold moved from 7.11% to 5.93%, with votes falling from 27,713,751 to 23,109,062. The change reflects routine securities lending and collateral movements across its custodial and asset management arms rather than a market sale.
