PC2: PC Gold appoints mine-builders Clark and Scully to board

PC Gold has appointed Rick Clark, former Red Back Mining CEO (sold to Kinross for US$7.1 billion), and Matthew Scully, a project director with over 20 years building mines for Perseus Mining and West African Resources, as Non-Executive Directors. Non-Executive Directors John Menzies and John Lewis have stepped down. The moves support the Prefeasibility Study underway for a 3Mtpa CIL plant at the 100%-owned Spring Hill Gold Project in the Northern Territory.

 
PRU
Perseus posts record profit, lifts dividend 87% on strong gold prices

Perseus Mining reported FY26 profit after tax of $480.5 million, up 14%, on revenue of $1.484 billion and EBITDA of $860.5 million. The board declared a 9.0 A$cps final dividend, taking the full year payout to a record 14 A$cps, up 87%, alongside completion of A$126 million of buybacks and a new A$350 million buyback programme. A further A$100 million distribution from the Meyas Sand sale proceeds is being considered, with Nyanzaga on track for first gold in January 2027.

Perseus lifts economic distribution to host nations in FY26 report

Perseus Mining's FY26 Sustainable Development Report shows US$1.19 billion in economic value distributed to host countries, up from US$813 million in FY25. Group TRIFR was 0.91 with zero lost time injuries across all operations. Emissions intensity improved to 0.52tCO2-e/oz from 0.56tCO2-e/oz in FY25, and 208.68ha of disturbed land was rehabilitated across five waste dump sites.

 
GML: Cowza strike grows to 4.5km, Celia South high-grade shoot found

Gateway's aircore drilling has extended continuous high-grade gold mineralisation at Cowza by a further 1km to approximately 4.5km, remaining open along strike and at depth. New step-out intercepts include 8m @ 2.1g/t Au from 84m (CSAC0066) and 8m @ 1.6g/t Au from 80m (CSAC0073), while Celia South returned 4m @ 9.7g/t Au from 60m (CSAC0101) across 1.5km of strike. RC drilling is ongoing at Cowza, with initial assay results due next month.

 
TOR: Torque hits multi-ounce visible gold intercept at HHH

Extensional RC drilling at the HHH deposit within Torque's newly renamed Ritz Gold Project has returned 11.0m @ 456g/t Au from 122m in hole 26HRC135, including 1.0m @ 3,625g/t Au, with abundant visible gold observed in drill chips. Follow-up diamond hole 26HDD012 returned 7.9m @ 1.37g/t Au from 146.2m, supporting a down-dip extension. HHH currently hosts a 73,000oz Inferred and Indicated resource at 2.0g/t Au, with two rigs continuing to drill across the project.

 
PDI: Predictive Discovery adopts new constitution as PDI Gold

Predictive Discovery Limited has lodged its new constitution as PDI Gold Limited, approved by shareholders at the General Meeting held on 21 August 2026. The document formalises the company's name change and standard governance provisions covering share capital, meetings, directors and winding up. No operational or financial details were included.

 
MAT: Matsa voluntarily suspended ahead of Devon Pit announcement

Matsa Resources has been suspended from quotation under Listing Rule 17.2 at its own request, effective before trading on 26 August 2026, to finalise an announcement on the Devon Pit Gold Project. Suspension continues until the earlier of the announcement's release or the start of trading on 31 August 2026.

 
OBM: Ora Banda posts record FY26 profit on Sand King ramp-up

Ora Banda Mining reported record FY26 revenue of $807.5 million, up from $404.3 million, with EBITDA up 134% to $431.1 million and NPAT up 16% to $216.2 million. Gold sold rose to 140,641oz (FY25: 93,076oz) as the Sand King Underground mine reached commercial production and achieved capital payback within 12 months of portal establishment. Closing cash reached $267.7 million, with an upsized $200 million revolving credit facility providing more than $468 million in total liquidity.

 
BGL: Bellevue appoints two independent non-executive directors

Bellevue Gold will appoint Debra Counsell and Tanya Rybarczyk as Independent Non-Executive Directors effective 23 September 2026, part of previously flagged board succession planning. Counsell was Chief Legal Officer and Company Secretary at BlueScope Steel, while Rybarczyk spent over 20 years at Wesfarmers including as CFO of WesCEF. Shannon Coates will not stand for re-election at this year's AGM.
