MRT: Phase 2 drilling extends high-grade Burbanks mineralisation

Maritana Minerals has received assays from the first 11 of 20 Phase 2 holes at its 100%-owned Burbanks Gold Project near Coolgardie, extending high-grade mineralisation below the Main Lode. Best intercepts include 0.48m @ 25.19g/t Au from 224.63m and 3.89m @ 2.25g/t Au from 299.11m (26HBBD056). Burbanks Underground, currently outside the Black Swan Processing Hub life-of-mine plan, is firming as potential future feed. Remaining Phase 2 assays and an updated Mineral Resource Estimate are expected in the September Quarter 2026.

 
GGP: Greatland delivers first full-year profit from Telfer

Greatland's first full financial year owning Telfer produced NPAT of A$862 million and EBITDA of A$1,332 million on revenue of A$2,259 million from 326,859oz gold and 14,730t copper sold. Free cash flow was A$737 million, lifting closing cash to A$1,289 million with total liquidity of A$1,764 million. FY27 guidance is 260,000 to 300,000oz gold at AISC of A$2,900 to A$3,330/oz, with construction starting at Havieron following its June 2026 FID.

 
BTR: Wide, high-grade infill hit at Two Mile Hill, Sandstone

Brightstar has returned 225.7m @ 3.11g/t Au from 284m in TMHRCD26006A, including 19m @ 24.3g/t Au from 306m and 1m @ 449g/t Au from 322m, from infill drilling at the Two Mile Hill-Shillington deposit. A second hole, TMHRCD26004, returned 194m @ 2.10g/t Au from 286m. The deposit hosts 14.6Mt @ 1.6g/t Au for 731koz Au, and results will feed into an updated Mineral Resource Estimate and pre-feasibility study, both due in November 2026.

 
PC2
PC Gold raises A$75 million to accelerate Spring Hill

PC Gold has secured firm commitments for a A$75 million single tranche institutional placement at A$1.05 per share, a 7.9% discount to the last close. The raise lifts pro-forma cash to approximately A$96.7 million, funding about 125,000m of drilling, underground infrastructure refurbishment and PFS/DFS work toward FID at Spring Hill. New shares settle 2 September 2026 and begin trading 3 September 2026.

PC Gold to issue shares to related party under LR10.11

PC Gold proposes to issue 1,904,762 shares at A$1.05 each, raising approximately A$2.0 million, to a party covered by Listing Rule 10.11. Shareholder approval is required and is estimated for 27 November 2026, with issue expected on 2 December 2026. Funds are earmarked for exploration, a PFS and DFS, developing the newly acquired camp, and working capital.

PC Gold prices placement following trading halt

PC Gold will issue 71,428,571 new shares at A$1.05 each, raising approximately A$75.0 million from institutional investors under its 15% and 10% placement capacities. Proceeds fund exploration and resource development, a PFS and DFS, development of a newly acquired camp, and working capital. Shares are due for issue on 3 September 2026.

 
CMM
Capricorn completes Big Springs divestment to Sentinel Metals

Capricorn and subsidiary Warriedar Resources have completed the sale of the 1.01Moz Big Springs Gold Project in Nevada to Sentinel Metals, receiving A$8.5 million cash and A$5.0 million in Sentinel shares (8,620,690 shares at A$0.58, escrowed 12 months). A further up to A$12.5 million in contingent consideration remains payable on resource, share price or transaction milestones.

Capricorn posts record FY26 profit, declares final dividend

Capricorn Metals delivered record FY26 underlying profit after tax of A$327.2 million, up 59% on FY25, on sales revenue of A$769.3 million from 123,096 ounces sold at an average A$6,241 per ounce. Karlawinda produced 123,589 ounces at AISC of A$1,629 per ounce, and net cash rose A$149.1 million to A$504.8 million. A 5 cent fully franked final dividend was declared, taking FY26 dividends to 10 cents per share, with FY27 guidance of 137,000 to 147,000 ounces at AISC of A$1,900 to A$2,100 per ounce as the Karlawinda Expansion Project completes commissioning.

Capricorn completes Big Springs divestment to Sentinel Metals

Capricorn has completed the sale of its non-core Big Springs gold project to Sentinel Metals, receiving A$13.7 million upfront (A$8.5 million cash plus a 6.2% Sentinel shareholding valued at ~A$5.2 million). A further A$12.5 million in deferred payments is contingent on resource milestones of 1.5Moz and 2.0Moz gold or related share price and divestment triggers. The sale sharpens focus on Karlawinda and Mt Gibson in WA.

 
EMR: Emerald voluntary sustainability report details safety, Dingo Range progress

Emerald's FY26 Sustainability Supplementary Report is a voluntary, unaudited disclosure alongside its statutory AASB S2 report. It notes a Group TRIFR 12-month moving average of 1.25 at 30 June 2026, Okvau's pour past 520koz of gold, and a Letter of Award to MACA Mining for Dingo Range with an estimated contract value of approximately A$562.5 million and mobilisation in December 2026.

 
TSO: Ternera resource classification upgraded, 78% now M&I

Tesoro's updated Ternera MRE stands at 51.6Mt at 1.10g/t Au for 1.82Moz, with 1.47Moz (78%) now classified Measured and Indicated, up 348koz on the August 2025 estimate. The unconstrained resource is 1.93Moz at 1.07g/t Au. The upgrade underpins a maiden Ore Reserve expected in September 2026, ahead of a DFS by year end.

 
GA8: GoldArc kicks off 6,600m discovery drilling at Leonora North

GoldArc has commenced an 84-hole, 6,600m RC program testing eight prospects near the 137,000oz Mt Stirling Deposit, six of which have never been drill tested. Rock chips up to 13.8g/t Au have been returned from historic workings near the targets, and six of the eight prospects sit within 1.5km of planned mining operations, allowing any discovery to be added to the mine plan. Drilling is expected to take four to six weeks, with first assays due from October 2026.

 
WAU: WA Gold begins resource infill drilling at Abercromby

WA Gold has commenced a 54-hole, ~7,200m RC drilling program targeting the open-pit component of Abercromby's Stage 1 Mine scenario, which carries a ~114,000oz production target and forecast pre-tax cashflow of A$270 million. The infill drilling aims to lift confidence in the existing 11.12Mt @ 1.45g/t Au for 518,000oz resource and support an updated MRE and maiden Ore Reserve. Assay results are expected from mid-September, with a Final Investment Decision targeted during 2027.

 
VAL: Drilling begins to firm up Boomerang resource confidence

Valiant has started an 8,000m Phase A diamond drilling program at its Boomerang gold deposit, part of the 100%-owned Reedy Gold Project, using two rigs on site. Boomerang hosts an existing open pit and underground resource of 5.26Mt @ 2.17g/t Au for 367koz, the largest single deposit at Reedy, and sits 45km by road from Westgold's Bluebird Mill under an Ore Purchase Agreement. The program aims to convert inferred resource to indicated and support evaluation of an accelerated open-pit opportunity, with assay results expected from the second quarter of FY27.

Corporate and funding: Greatland Resources delivered its first full financial year owning Telfer, with NPAT of A$862 million, EBITDA of A$1,332 million and free cash flow of A$737 million lifting closing cash to A$1,289 million; FY27 guidance is 260,000 to 300,000oz gold at AISC of A$2,900 to A$3,330/oz. Drilling and resources: Brightstar's infill drilling at Two Mile Hill-Shillington returned 225.7m @ 3.11g/t Au from 284m including 1m @ 449g/t Au from 322m, feeding into a November 2026 MRE and PFS update. WA Gold, Valiant and GoldArc all commenced new drilling programs at Abercromby, Boomerang and Leonora North respectively, targeting resource conversion, open-pit upside and greenfields discovery.

Studies and guidance: Capricorn's FY27 guidance of 137,000 to 147,000oz at AISC of A$1,900 to A$2,100/oz reflects commissioning of the Karlawinda Expansion Project, while Greatland's Havieron construction start follows its June 2026 FID.
