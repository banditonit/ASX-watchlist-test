AKN: AuKing supplements Tundulu diamond hole report with mineral estimates

AuKing has lodged a compliance supplement to its 1 September 2026 release on hole 26NH023 at Tundulu, adding visual mineral abundance estimates as required under ASX Compliance Update 04.23. Logged intervals at approximately 368 to 370.5m and 381m show apatite calcite carbonatite with calcite up to ~75%, apatite up to ~10% and pyrite up to ~5%. No mineralisation has been reported, pending assay results, which are expected within 5 to 6 weeks.
