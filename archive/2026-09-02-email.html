TRE: Toubani outlines path to first gold production in 2027

Toubani Resources released an investor presentation setting out its strategy for delivering first gold in 2027, referencing the definitive feasibility study dated 31 March 2025. No new financial or production figures were disclosed beyond those previously outlined. Next catalyst is continued project execution following the recent gold stream drawdown.

 
CYL: Infill drilling confirms bulk-scale, high-grade Cinnamon zone

Catalyst Metals' infill drilling at Cinnamon, part of the Plutonic Gold Belt, has returned true width intercepts of 27m @ 20g/t Au and 57m @ 6.9g/t Au, confirming continuity of the high-grade shoot beneath the existing open pit. Assays also confirmed mineralisation in parallel, deeper conglomerate units, including 8m @ 4.7g/t Au. A maiden Resource and Reserve for Cinnamon, expected to become Plutonic's sixth ore source, is due in coming weeks.

 
GPR: Woodlark drilling extends gold mineralisation across multiple prospects

Geopacific has completed a 29-hole RC and diamond drilling program across seven prospects at its 1.98Moz Woodlark Gold Project in Papua New Guinea. Best results include 36.0m @ 1.48g/t Au from 60.0m at Woodlark King NW, 2.3m @ 16.40g/t Au from 114.7m at Little MacKenzie, and 1.4m @ 29.40g/t Au from 257.0m at Busai Deeps. The company says results offer potential upside to the recently completed DFS, which had a post-tax NPV of A$1.3 billion. Next steps involve geological model updates and continued regional soil and auger sampling.

 
KAL: RC drilling extends high-grade gold at Lighthorse, Pinjin

Follow-up RC drilling of 19 holes for 3,194m at the Lighthorse prospect returned the highest-grade result to date, 19.70g/t Au from 145m to 146m in KGRC26036, within a broader intercept of 3m @ 9.01g/t Au from 145m. Drilling also identified a second, northern supergene blanket and thick mineralised envelopes up to 27m. Further drilling is planned to test newly defined targets, with aircore results from the LTZ Flexure area still pending.

 
SKM: M'Basso mineralised strike extended to nearly 2km

Phase 2 RC drilling at the M'Basso Prospect, Zaranou Gold Project in Côte d'Ivoire, has extended the mineralised footprint to approximately 2km of strike, confirming continuity of the principal zones defined in Phase 1. Best new results include 45m @ 0.72g/t Au from 125m (ZARC0185) and 14m @ 1.32g/t Au from 55m (ZARC0195). A Mineral Resource Estimate upgrade for M'Basso is targeted for early in the fourth quarter of 2026, with Ehuasso RC results to follow later in the quarter.

 
GML: Fresh rock RC hole confirms high-grade discovery at Cowza

Gateway's first RC hole to test the Cowza structure in fresh rock returned 41m @ 2.0g/t Au from 104m, including 9m @ 7.5g/t Au from 105m, sitting directly beneath an earlier aircore hit of 8m @ 1.9g/t Au. A second hole, CZRC054, missed the target contact but still returned 52m @ 0.3g/t Au from 44m, extending the fertile zone at least 2km further north. Diamond rigs are due at Cowza later this month, and the company separately banked A$8.23 million from liquidating its securities portfolio.

 
STK: Strickland announces MD and Chair succession as Rogozna advances

Managing Director Paul L'Herpiniere will step down once a successor is found, as Strickland shifts from exploration toward development of its Rogozna gold and base metals project in Serbia. Chairman Anthony McClure will step aside as Chair on 1 October 2026, remaining a Non-Executive Director, with Sandra Bates taking over as Chair. Non-Executive Director Trent Franklin will leave the Board at the November 2026 AGM.

 
MRT: Maritana advances Black Swan hub construction, camp expansion complete

Maritana Minerals reports Early Works ramping up at the Black Swan Processing Hub, with demolition, bulk earthworks and long-lead procurement (pre-leach thickener, SAG and ball mill motors) underway. The second-stage construction camp expansion of 57 rooms is complete, and the permanent 60-room camp (scalable to 120 rooms) is expected operational from January 2027. The Clearing Permit for CIL earthworks is progressing through final approval. First gold production remains targeted for H2 CY2027.

 
VAL: Valiant accelerates Comet Gold Mine restart works

Valiant Gold has brought forward key restart workstreams at its 100%-owned Comet Gold Mine after favourable underground conditions were found on re-entry in July. A 6.1MW power station, primary ventilation, dewatering equipment and a scalable 30-room camp have all been ordered, alongside a tender for underground grade control and resource definition drilling. Completion of the Comet Restart Study, and a preliminary restart mining schedule, is targeted for Q2 FY27.

 
MAT: Matsa remains suspended pending Devon Pit update and funding proposal

Matsa Resources' voluntary suspension, sought pending an update on the Devon Pit Gold Project and a funding proposal, continues past its 2 September 2026 deadline. The company states it is not yet able to release an announcement that would allow reinstatement. Trading stays halted under Listing Rule 17.3 until ASX is satisfied MAT complies with Listing Rule 3.1.

 
MEK: Meeka's plant reaches 600ktpa nameplate throughput

Meeka Metals' processing plant consistently operated at an annualised rate of ~600ktpa through July and August 2026, hitting 630ktpa in August. The ore sorter, installed to double the head grade of Andy Well ore and lift treatable run-of-mine material to ~800ktpa through the existing 600ktpa mill, is on track to begin commissioning in early October 2026 with ~50kt of development ore already stockpiled.

 
NVO: Novo reports routine monthly CDI movement

Novo's CDI count fell to 136,942,081 at end of August 2026, down 250,000 from 137,192,081, after 250,000 CDIs transmuted to TSX common shares. Common shares on issue rose to 297,160,438, up 1,750,000, including 1,500,000 new TSX shares issued. Options (3,000,000) and warrants (8,200,000) were unchanged.
