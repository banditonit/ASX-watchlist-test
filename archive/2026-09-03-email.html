VAU: Vault lifts group Ore Reserves 9% to 3.9 million ounces gold

Vault's 30 June 2026 statement reports Group Ore Reserves of 3.9 million ounces of gold, up 9% year-on-year after FY26 mine depletion of 398,800 ounces, with Mineral Resources broadly steady at 12.1 million ounces. Leonora Ore Reserves rose 7% to 2.7 million ounces on Darlot growth and KoTH underground replacement, Deflector underground Ore Reserves grew 58% net of depletion to 141,000 ounces, and Mount Monger Ore Reserves increased 15% to 602,000 ounces. Reserves underpin a three-year standalone production outlook to FY29, with FY27 exploration investment of $44 million and 187km of approved drilling.

EL8: Elevate's U-pgrade pilot plant matches bench-scale results at Marenica

Stages 1 to 4 of the U-pgradeTM pilot plant rejected approximately 88% of original ore mass while recovering 79% of uranium, upgrading Marenica ore from 89ppm U to 590ppm U, a 7 times upgrade ratio in line with 2013 bench-scale test work. Uranium names Cauldron, Paladin and Infini all reported strike extensions or expanded programs, while PolarX's visual massive sulphide hits at Caribou Dome await October assays.
