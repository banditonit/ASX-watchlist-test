JAV: Javelin begins deep diamond drilling at Coogee gold-copper targets

Javelin Minerals has commenced a two-hole diamond program at the Coogee Gold-Copper Project, testing the Coogee North and Coogee West intrusive targets, each hole planned to 800m to 1,000m over a 5 to 6 week program. The targets are untested deep magnetic anomalies sitting beneath the existing Coogee North resource of 3.6Mt at 1.08g/t Au and 1.0Mt at 0.41% Cu. Drilling is co-funded via the WA Government's Exploration Incentive Scheme.

ASN: Anson secures $194 million Utah tax incentive for Green River

The Utah Inland Port Authority has approved a business incentive capped at $193,995,683 for Anson's Green River Lithium Project, calculated as 50% of projected property tax differential over 25 years (about US$8 million a year). The rebate begins once the project is operating and Anson is also exploring an alternative bond-financing structure with UIPA to fund utility and transport infrastructure. The Green River plant, targeting 10,000tpa lithium carbonate, was scoped in March 2026 at $569 million total capital cost; a separate GOED tax reduction application is due before its board on 10 September 2026.

EPM: Mineralogy work backs NdPr flotation route at Grønnedal

Quantitative mineralogy by ALS Metallurgy on four Ivigtût composites shows 87.5% to 94.3% of contained NdPr sits in identified REE-bearing minerals, mainly synchysite and calcioancylite-type carbonates and fluorocarbonates. Combined density and magnetic separation on one composite removed 39% of mass while retaining about 70% of TREEs, an upgrade factor of 1.3 to roughly 12,700ppm TREE. Next steps are rougher and cleaner flotation testing followed by hydrometallurgical trials on a rough concentrate.
