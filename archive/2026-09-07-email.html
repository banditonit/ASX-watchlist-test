LGM: Legacy expands scoping study scope after 39% resource growth at Mt Carrington

Legacy Minerals says its Optimised Scoping Study with Ausenco and WSP is progressing well, assessing a larger processing throughput above the original 1Mtpa base case. The study builds on the July 2026 Mineral Resource Estimate of 47.9Mt at 1.0g/t AuEq for 1.6Moz AuEq, up 39% in tonnes and 69% in Indicated Resources (19.3Mt) since the prior estimate. Study results are expected in the December 2026 quarter.

Legacy Minerals says its Optimised Scoping Study with Ausenco and WSP is progressing well, assessing a larger processing throughput above the original 1Mtpa base case.

 
BTR: Brightstar hits bonanza grade at Bull Oak, Sandstone

Infill RC drilling at the Bull Oak deposit within the 2.9Moz Sandstone Gold Project returned 7m @ 119g/t Au from 199m, including 1m @ 751g/t Au, the highest single assay yet recorded at Sandstone. Bull Oak hosts 10.5Mt @ 1.0g/t Au for 322koz Au and remains open at depth, with geology comparable to the larger Two Mile Hill deposit. Results will feed a resource upgrade ahead of the Sandstone Pre-Feasibility Study, targeted before the end of 2026, with three rigs still turning.

RXL: Youanmi construction and mining on schedule for mid-CY2027 gold

Rox Resources reports continued on-schedule progress at its 100%-owned Youanmi Gold Mine, with the 1.0Mtpa processing plant advancing (screen plant, crusher foundation, ball mill and ISAMill foundations poured) and the tailings dam under construction. Mining exceeded feasibility study rates with 577m mined in August, including 431m of development advance. The 351-room accommodation camp is complete, and production stoping is anticipated to commence in October.

TOK: Tolu fully funded to restart Tolukuma Gold Mine

Tolu has accepted a K95 million (approximately A$30 million) five-year debt facility from National Banking Corporation at 9.60% per annum, secured over company assets. Combined with approximately A$50 million cash at 30 June 2026, this fully funds remaining capital works at Tolukuma, targeting production in Q1 2027. An updated Mineral Resource Estimate is expected within 14 days, and infrastructure contract announcements are due in Q4 2026.

GBR: More high-grade hits extend Peak Hill mineralisation

New assays from Great Boulder's 40,000m Peak Hill program show high-grade gold at Golden Treasure, Durack and Enigma, including 12m @ 8.56g/t Au from 8m and 8m @ 9.00g/t Au from 76m at Golden Treasure. Durack mineralisation is now traced over 300m of strike, open north and at depth, against an existing resource of 2.89Mt @ 1.2g/t Au for 112,000oz. First assays from Five Ways are expected shortly, ahead of a planned resource upgrade by year end.

I88: Drilling commences on ~5,000m Portland Creek uranium program

Infini Resources has started diamond drilling at its Portland Creek Uranium Project in Newfoundland, Canada, kicking off the expanded approximately 5,000m Phase 3 program flagged on 31 August 2026. Targeting is based on 3D magnetic inversion modelling identifying structurally controlled hydrothermal alteration, including the first test of demagnetised granite at the Falls Lake Prospect, where soil sampling previously returned assays up to approximately 7.5% U3O8. An approximately 1,000m trenching program is running in parallel to refine drill targeting, with results from Phase 3 the next catalyst.
