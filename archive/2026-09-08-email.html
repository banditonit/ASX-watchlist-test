PTN: New 1.5km J25 gold target defined at Pine Creek

In-fill surface sampling has delineated a coherent 1.5km-long gold anomaly at the new J25 prospect along the Western Shear Zone, with results up to 0.99g/t Au. Sampling also refined additional targets at Burnside SW and Mt Margaret. Four drill rigs remain active testing the pipeline, with about 500 samples from the Jessops area still outstanding.

AAR: Astral drills more high-grade infill hits at Theia Stage 1, Mandilla

Astral reported assays for 41 more infill RC holes (4,456m) at the Theia deposit within the Stage 1 open pit at 100%-owned Mandilla, with a weighted average grade of 1.54g/t Au against the Stage 1 MRE grade of 1.1g/t Au. Best hits included 7m @ 10.4g/t Au from 133m (AMRC446) and 4m @ 13.5g/t Au from 106m (AMRC410). A separate RC hole with diamond tail, AMRCD374, collared inside the pit returned 1m @ 219g/t Au from 25m before being terminated early at 318.9m due to deviation. The Stage 1 infill program has now completed 225 of 431 planned holes, with a further 206 holes still to drill.

MRT: Encore FID on Windarra Tailings brings payment and royalty to Maritana

Encore Minerals has made a positive Final Investment Decision on the Windarra Tailings Project and exercised its option to proceed to the Development Period, triggering a non-refundable A$400,000 payment to subsidiary Poseidon Nickel. Encore funds all development using Draslovka's glycine leaching technology, while Maritana retains a 3% net smelter return royalty (1.5% on Lancefield tailings) and is due a further A$1.6 million rehabilitation bond contribution on execution of the full form agreement. Total cash payments to date under the agreement reach A$1.25 million.

PRX: PhotonAssay check assays confirm prior Fire Assay grades at Tanami North

Prodigy Gold's PhotonAssay analysis of 27 high-grade RC samples from 12 drill holes at Hyperion and Tregony averaged 11.57g/t Au versus 11.56g/t Au by original Fire Assay, an R² of approximately 98.8%. The best interval, TGRC25007 from 59m to 62m, averaged 29.4g/t Au by Fire Assay and 28.9g/t Au by PhotonAssay, with a single sample as high as 41.6g/t Au. Fire Assay remains the primary method for resource estimation, with PhotonAssay used as a QAQC check.

REC: Tungsten re-assays point to major Cu-W-Mo system at Brandy Hill South

Re-assaying of pulps from two 2022 diamond holes using lithium borate fusion returned tungsten grades up to 49% higher than the original 4-acid ICP-MS method, which had under-reported WO3. Best results include 7.5m @ 1.65% Cu and 0.13% WO3 from 375.5m and 11m @ 0.33% Cu and 0.15% WO3 from 124m, both coincident with existing Cu-Mo mineralisation defined over 500m strike and 400m depth. More than 1,000 pulps have been flagged for priority re-assay, and drone magnetics start in September to test a 2.5km geophysical target.

Day in Brief
 

Trading halts: PolarX has suspended trading pending assay results from current drilling at Caribou Dome, Alaska, expected back by 9 September 2026, building on its 2 September report of massive sulphide with visible chalcopyrite. Antares Metals is halted pending a material announcement on a capital raise, with resumption due by 10 September 2026 and no terms yet released.

Drilling and extensions: Astral's 41-hole infill program at Theia (Mandilla) returned a weighted average of 1.54g/t Au, well above the 1.1g/t Au Stage 1 MRE grade, headlined by 7m at 10.4g/t Au from 133m and a standout 1m at 219g/t Au from 25m before early termination. Titan extended the high-grade zone at Cerro Verde's Brecha-Comanche target with 16.0m at 2.8g/t Au from 433.9m confirming a 30m lateral extension, adding a fourth diamond rig ahead of an early-2027 resource update. Yandal pushed its Domain C zone at Arrakis a further 80m down-plunge with 24.2m at 1.4g/t Au from 237.8m, still open at depth, and Hot Chili reported 398m at 0.45% CuEq from surface at La Verde including a high-grade 62m core, feeding a maiden MRE and revised Costa Fuego PFS. Pine Creek defined a new 1.5km gold anomaly at the J25 target with four rigs still active, and Revenue Mining's tungsten re-assays at Brandy Hill South, using lithium borate fusion, lifted WO3 grades up to 49% versus the original method, pointing to an under-reported Cu-W-Mo system ahead of September drone magnetics.

Corporate and funding: Encore Minerals' positive FID on Windarra Tailings triggers a A$400,000 payment to Maritana subsidiary Poseidon Nickel, a retained 3% net smelter return royalty and a further A$1.6 million rehabilitation bond contribution, taking total payments to date to A$1.25 million. Lotus Resources set 9 September 2026 as the issue date for its A$35.0 million convertible notes (converting at A$0.253 per share) and for 66,287,879 associated warrants exercisable at A$0.8500 per share, both tied to shareholder approval obtained 2 September 2026. Noronex applied to quote 88,843,956 new shares issued as non-cash consideration and facilitation fee for the Sleitat Project acquisition, lifting total shares on issue to 736,484,805. Revolver Resources confirmed no undisclosed information behind a share price spike from A$0.052 to A$0.067, and Piche Resources began a strategic portfolio review alongside a search for a new CEO, retaining its Cerro Chacon, Ashburton and Sierra Cuadrada projects.

Studies and approvals: Barton Gold's Tunkillia project cleared South Australia's environmental Scoping Report approval, setting terms of reference for Mining Lease and development approvals ahead of a pending updated JORC resource and a targeted Q1 CY27 Ore Reserve and Pre-Feasibility Study. Prodigy Gold's PhotonAssay check assays at Tanami North confirmed prior Fire Assay grades (11.57g/t Au versus 11.56g/t Au, R² approximately 98.8%), validating the QAQC process without changing resource methodology.

Quarterly: Elmenteh Minerals' half-year filing showed a narrower net loss of A$15.7 million (from A$11.8 million in H2'25) and cash of A$41.7 million at 30 June 2026 (up from A$31.8 million), with A$1.8 million of the roughly A$9.28 million Penouta acquisition cap now advanced.
