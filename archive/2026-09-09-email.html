CYL: Catalyst grows Keillor Resource 280% on Plutonic Belt

Catalyst Metals has lifted the Keillor (formerly K2) Resource by ~280% to 3.2Mt at 3.0g/t Au for 307koz, up from 81koz at 3.6g/t Au, after its first material drilling in 25 years. Indicated underground ounces rose ~500% to 187koz, supporting an extension beyond the initial three year mine life. First ore was mined in the June 2026 quarter, and an updated Reserve is due in coming weeks as part of Catalyst's push toward a 2Moz Reserve base and ±200koz annual production.

STN: Saturn presents Apollo Hill scale and PFS economics at conference

Saturn Metals used the RRS Gold Coast Conference to showcase Apollo Hill, a 2.83Moz gold resource with a maiden 1.59Moz ore reserve and a PFS-modelled NPV(8%) of A$973 million over a 14 year mine life. Cash stood at A$80.6 million as at 31 August 2026. Next catalysts are a reserve upgrade and DFS delivery targeted for CY2026.

WGX: Westgold guides FY27 production, sets FY29 target near 500koz

Westgold's updated Three-Year Outlook lifts Group gold production from 385koz to 425koz in FY27 to 460koz to 510koz by FY29, with AISC falling to A$2,640 to A$3,000/oz by FY29. Growth capital of A$450 million to A$480 million in FY27 funds Cue and Meekatharra hub expansions and accelerated underground development, lifting processing capacity to over 7Mtpa. The Fletcher Zone at Beta Hunt, excluded from the outlook, is flagged as potential upside toward 600,000ozpa.

RXL: Rox lifts Youanmi resource and reserve in annual update

Rox Resources' annual update puts the Youanmi Mineral Resource at 2,195koz as at 30 June 2026, up from 2,170koz a year earlier, with a net 4% shift from inferred to indicated on infill drilling. The Ore Reserve rose 8% to 727koz (from 674koz) after depletion of 774oz, using a conservative A$3,200/oz gold price against spot of around A$6,100/oz. Results from the recent Interceptor surface program and underground grade control drilling fell after the cutoff and will feed future updates, alongside a potential resource review at Commonwealth.

BTR: Goldfields construction tracking to plan, first gold June 2027

Brightstar's 1.5Mtpa Laverton processing plant remains on schedule and within budget for first gold in June 2027. CIL circuit concrete is complete and tank installation has begun ahead of schedule, and the first mechanical equipment (a vibrating screen) has arrived on site. Underground mining at Second Fortune has concluded into care and maintenance, leaving stockpiles of ~50kt @ 3.1g/t Au for ~5koz as ramp-up feed. The Goldfields Project targets ~75koz p.a. production from FY28 over an initial six-year mine life, with Lord Byron open-pit mobilisation targeted for the December 2026 quarter.
