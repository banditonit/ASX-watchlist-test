KCN: Nueva Esperanza reserve upgrade extends mine life past 12 years

Kingsgate's updated Nueva Esperanza (Chile) Ore Reserves rose 31% to 51.9 million ounces of silver and 37% to 0.26 million ounces of gold, at US$35/oz silver and US$2,500/oz gold, lifting reserve life beyond 12 years at a 2Mtpa processing rate. Mineral Resources also grew to 62.4 million ounces of silver and 0.36 million ounces of gold. The increase reflects higher price assumptions and reduced royalty costs following the 2026 Anglo American royalty purchase.

ODY: New high-grade lode and Argos extension at Cable West, Tuckanarra

RC drilling west of the Cable deposit at Tuckanarra has returned 5m @ 16.1g/t Au from 30m, including 2m @ 36.7g/t Au, and 9m @ 6.8g/t Au from 99m in hole CBRC0451, identifying a new mineralised structure not in the current geological model. Infill drilling has also confirmed and extended the Argos lode beneath historical laterite mineralisation, with 5m @ 6.7g/t Au from 18m (CBRC0440) and 7m @ 2.6g/t Au from 42m (CBRC0441). Results are from 18 of 60 pending holes from a 20,000m program, with follow-up drilling planned to step further west across a 3km by 1km target area before an updated resource and Stage 1 Feasibility Study.

GML: Gateway defines new 3km Pewy gold target on Cowza trend

Gateway Mining has identified Pewy, a 3km-long demagnetised structural corridor south of Ward along the Celia Shear, sharing the geological hallmarks seen at Cowza before its discovery hole of 41m @ 2.0g/t Au from 104m. Flanking aircore results of 8m @ 1.0g/t Au from 84m (SCAC0003) and 4m @ 1.4g/t Au from 48m (WAAC0230) bracket the untested core, which remains completely undrilled. First-pass aircore drilling is scheduled for early October, funded by the recently completed A$45 million placement plus a A$15.1 million cash and liquids balance at 30 June 2026.

WGX: Westgold sets three-year outlook to ~500kozpa by FY29

RXL: Rox starts resource conversion and growth drilling at Youanmi

WSR: Soil sampling defines three gold targets at Jerrys Bore

UltraFine+ soil sampling across 1,167 samples at the Jerrys Bore project, 165km north of Laverton, has outlined three high-priority gold targets (T1 to T3) plus a fourth secondary target. Anomalism is defined by coincident gold with pathfinder elements As, Bi, Mo, Sb and Te, with target strike lengths of more than 2km each. Aircore drilling is planned to test the targets.

JAV: Deep diamond hole progressing at Coogee North gold-copper target

Javelin's first diamond hole testing the Coogee North intrusive gold-copper target is drilling ahead on schedule, currently at 150m depth toward a planned total depth of 800 to 1,000m. The modelled magnetic anomaly is expected to be intersected at approximately 550m depth. Coogee sits on a granted Mining Lease with a JORC Resource of 3.6Mt at 1.08g/t Au for 127,000oz gold and 1.0Mt at 0.41% Cu for 4,122t copper. The 5 to 6 week campaign, co-funded under the WA Government Exploration Incentive Scheme, will next drill the Coogee West target.

Day in Brief
 

Corporate and funding: St Barbara's Simberi sale to Lingbao Gold Group for A$410 million cash plus A$43 million capital repayment was the day's largest transaction, alongside Bannerman's completed A$124 million placement at A$4.00 per share to fund Etango through construction. Antares Metals detailed a two-tranche A$2.57 million placement for Quinns and Mt Isa North drilling, Alligator Energy closed a A$2.121 million SPP for Blackbush, Plumbush and Samphire work, and Kalgoorlie Gold Mining halted trading to plan a capital raising, expected to resume 14 September 2026.

Reserves and resources: Kingsgate lifted Nueva Esperanza Ore Reserves 31% to 51.9 million ounces silver and 37% to 0.26 million ounces gold, extending mine life past 12 years, while Westgold's Group Ore Reserve rose 41% in FY26 to 4.1Moz underpinning new FY27 guidance of 385koz to 425koz and a path to approximately 500kozpa by FY29.

Drilling and targets: Ora Banda's step-out drilling south of Waihi defined a new high-grade prospect, Midnight, including 11.5m @ 26.0g/t Au and 1.6m @ 65.9g/t Au, as underground mining began at Waihi itself. Odyssey Gold reported a new mineralised structure at Cable West (5m @ 16.1g/t Au from 30m) and an extended Argos lode at Tuckanarra, Gateway Mining outlined an untested 3km Pewy target sharing hallmarks of its Cowza discovery, and Gold 8 (GG8) extended Lakeview at depth (7.0m @ 12.6g/t Au from 392.7m) while securing the final Sovereign underground permit and strong 94.6% gold extraction metallurgy.

Studies and development: Legacy Minerals recapped Mt Carrington's A$514 million pre-tax NPV scoping study and active Mascotte and Emu drilling, QMines engaged BurnVoir to arrange Mt Chalmers development funding, and Rox Resources began resource conversion drilling at Youanmi following its 2,195koz Mineral Resource update.

Other: West African Resources posted record H1 2026 NPAT of A$437 million and declared a 20 cent unfranked special dividend, Alara Resources found no explanation for a share price and volume spike, and Cosmo Metals was granted a new NSW copper licence with a 4.69% Cu rock chip result.
