BC8: Extensional drilling extends Trojan mineralised footprint

Black Cat's 56-hole RC program at the 100%-owned Trojan deposit, part of Kal East, intersected mineralisation beyond the current 202koz @ 1.1g/t Au Resource in both the northern down-dip zone and the southeastern flat-lying oxide zone. Best results included 3m @ 6.88g/t Au from 142m (26TRRC008A) and 7m @ 3.02g/t Au from 257m (26TRRC010A). Further extensional and infill drilling is planned to support a Resource upgrade and updated life of mine planning.

MRT: Burbanks resource jumps 62% to 755koz, group total tops 2.1Moz

Maritana's updated Burbanks Gold Project Mineral Resource has grown to 13.7Mt at 1.7g/t Au for 755koz, up 290koz from the prior 6.1Mt at 2.4g/t Au for 466koz estimate, lifting company-wide resources above 2.1Moz. Growth was driven almost entirely by Burbanks North, now 11.1Mt at 1.47g/t Au for 523koz, with the Indicated component rising to 29%. Remaining Phase 2 assays are due in the September quarter 2026, ahead of a further resource update in the December quarter 2026 and follow-on Ore Reserve studies.

ALY: Alchemy starts RC drilling at NSW gold-copper targets

Alchemy Resources has commenced RC drilling at its 80%-owned Yellow Mountain and Overflow project in NSW, with up to 15 holes planned. Yellow Mountain will test the northern extension of 2025 intercepts of 113m @ 1.17% CuEq (YMRC004) and 31m @ 1.54% CuEq (YMRC005), plus two IP-defined targets including a 750m anomaly east of prior drilling. Overflow drilling targets high-grade gold shoots and the northern extension of the existing 342koz AuEq resource beneath shallow cover. Results are expected in due course.

Anson secures further A$212 million Utah tax credit

Utah's Governor's Office of Economic Development has approved a post-performance tax credit of approximately A$212 million for the Green River Lithium Project, under the REDTIF program, equal to 50% of projected state tax revenue of approximately A$425 million over 20 years. Combined with the earlier Utah Inland Port Authority rebate, total incentives now stand at US$406,514,271 (A$569,119,979). The financial impact will be assessed in the Green River Definitive Feasibility Study.

Day in Brief
 

Resources and reserves: Maritana Resources' updated Burbanks Mineral Resource rose 62% to 13.7Mt at 1.7g/t Au for 755koz, driven by Burbanks North's growth to 11.1Mt at 1.47g/t Au for 523koz, taking group-wide resources past 2.1Moz. Black Cat Syndicate reported RC drilling at Trojan extending mineralisation beyond the existing 202koz @ 1.1g/t Au Resource in both the northern down-dip and southeastern oxide zones, with best results of 3m @ 6.88g/t Au from 142m and 7m @ 3.02g/t Au from 257m.

Drilling: Alchemy Resources began RC drilling at its Yellow Mountain and Overflow project in NSW, testing extensions of prior high-grade copper-gold intercepts including 113m @ 1.17% CuEq and 31m @ 1.54% CuEq, alongside the 342koz AuEq Overflow resource. Stavely Minerals used the Resources Rising Stars conference to recap its Thursday's Gossan scoping study economics (A$818 million pre-tax NPV, 40% IRR, 60Mt at 0.58% CuEq) without new drilling or financial disclosures.

Corporate and funding: Anson Resources secured a second Utah incentive, a post-performance tax credit of approximately A$212 million, taking combined state incentives for the Green River Lithium Project to US$406,514,271 (A$569,119,979), with the financial impact to be assessed in the Green River DFS. Manhattan Gold entered a trading halt pending a material capital raising announcement, expected to resume by 15 September 2026 or the announcement, whichever comes first. American Uranium completed its A$1.235 million Convertible Loan Note raising to fund Lo Herma ISR Uranium Project studies, with a general meeting to follow on facility approvals and a further A$1.3 million note tranche.

Quarterlies: Six recurring filings were lodged, including Gilgama Resources' DFS economics and Hawthorn Resources' maiden Lincoln JORC estimate of 2.46Mt at 5.1g/t Au for 402koz, alongside half-year losses from Galan Lithium subsidiaries, Sunstone Metals, Tesoro Resources and Alvo Minerals.
