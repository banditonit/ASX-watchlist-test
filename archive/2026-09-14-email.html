CYL: Trident underground Reserve grows 32% to 524koz

Catalyst Metals lifted the Trident underground Probable Reserve to 3.9Mt at 4.2g/t Au for 524koz, up 32%, supporting a circa10 year mine life at 60-80koz per annum. Inferred Resources of 448koz remain for further infill conversion, following the June 2026 Resource update of 1.1Moz at 5.4g/t Au. First ore is expected in CY2027, feeding the underutilised 2Mtpa Plutonic CIL plant.

KAL: KalGold raises A$3.4 million to fund Pinjin exploration push

KalGold has secured firm commitments for a A$2.3 million placement at $0.02 per share, a 13.0% discount to last close, alongside a fully underwritten 1 for 10 entitlement offer to raise a further A$1.1 million. Proceeds will fund further drilling at the Lighthorse discovery within the Pinjin Gold Project, including follow-up RC drilling and first-pass aircore testing of new targets. The entitlement offer closes 6 October 2026.

WGR: WGR pivots to alternative processing after Wiluna mill deferral

Western Gold Resources is engaging alternative processing counterparties and assessing a company-controlled processing solution after Wiluna Mining deferred third-party tolling at its mill. The Expanded Scoping Study is being revised on the updated 4.84Mt @ 1.8g/t Au resource for 277koz, aiming to lift tonnes, ounces and mine life beyond the current 14-month Stage 1 plan. The Board is also weighing potential corporate opportunities, with no certainty any transaction proceeds.

Day in Brief
 

Resources and reserves: Catalyst Metals lifted the Trident underground Probable Reserve 32% to 524koz (3.9Mt at 4.2g/t Au), supporting a circa 10 year mine life, with 448koz Inferred remaining for conversion. Sinclair Gold doubled its Mt Henry Gold Project Resource to 1.8Moz (48Mt at 1.2g/t Au), with 1.3Moz in Measured and Indicated and 90% of ounces within 200m of surface, five rigs still active and 25 step-out assays pending.

Drilling: Youanmi's Rare X reported high-grade underground infill at United North, including 10.30m @ 12.46g/t Au from 184.70m and 1.56m @ 43.42g/t Au from 185.00m, with ore drive development now roughly 125m beyond the DFS mine plan and production stoping expected within weeks. GreenTech's Ayshia diamond hole returned 4.76m @ 7.13% Cu, 1.20% Zn, 0.88g/t Au and 29.6g/t Ag from 367m, extending mineralisation 65m down plunge beyond the current Resource envelope, ahead of a Q4 2026 Resource update. AuKing's maiden Tundulu REE assays returned 91m @ 1.51% TREO from 40m and 80m @ 1.54% TREO from surface with unusual heavy rare earth enrichment, while Connected Minerals began diamond drilling at Bailundo targeting niobium across a roughly 6,000m programme.

Corporate and funding: Minerals 260 secured an additional $200 million Franco-Nevada package ($170 million for a royalty increase to 3.90%, plus a $30 million equity subscription commitment), lifting total Franco-Nevada investment to $420 million, but remains halted pending the equity raising announcement. Atomic Eagle's largest shareholder locked in early exercise of options for approximately A$10.9 million by 28 October 2026, lifting available cash to about A$23.0 million and funding Zambia and Niger uranium work through 2028 without near-term dilution. KalGold raised A$3.4 million via placement and entitlement offer to fund further Pinjin drilling, while Javelin Minerals and White Cliff Minerals both entered halts pending change of control and strategic investor announcements respectively.

Studies and processing: Cyprium applied acid to the Nifty heaps for the first time since 2009, with the SX plant substantially complete and first cathode now expected in the fourth quarter. Western Gold Resources is revising its Expanded Scoping Study after Wiluna Mining deferred third-party mill tolling, exploring alternative processing and potential corporate options on its 277koz Wiluna resource.

Quarterlies: Eleven recurring filings were lodged, led by Kalamazoo's Ashburton Resource update (1.44Moz) and Mt Olympus scoping study (NPV8 A$423 million), alongside Genesis Minerals' 1.5Moz WA resource and Thomson Resources' Dynasty update to 3.9Moz Au, none changing guidance.
