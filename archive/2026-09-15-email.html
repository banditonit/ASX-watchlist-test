EVN
Evolution lifts FY27 exploration spend and drilling metres

Evolution's investor briefing sets out FY27 discovery plans across Cowal, Northparkes and Ernest Henry, with group exploration guidance of A$130 million to A$160 million and 360km to 400km of planned drilling, up from A$88 million and 260km in FY26. Cowal targets a new underground mine and E41 expansion, Northparkes an aggressive 20km open pit program following maiden Major Tom and E51 Mineral Resources, and Ernest Henry a 2.5km deep directional drilling program to test main orebody extensions at depth.

Evolution details project pipeline and Tier 1 growth studies

Evolution's investor briefing outlines four projects in execution: Cowal's A$430 million Open Pit Continuation, Northparkes' A$545 million E22 block cave (first ore end FY30), a A$75 million coarse particle flotation upgrade targeting about 2% recovery uplift and 8Mtpa throughput, and the A$160 million Bert project at Ernest Henry for first ore in FY29. Cowal is framed around a 10Moz mineral resource and pathway to a Tier 1 asset, while Northparkes carries a 630Mt mineral resource. Studies at Northparkes' expansion, including MJH and E44, are due for completion in FY27.

Evolution details capital management and record shareholder returns

Evolution's CFO investor presentation highlights a record annual dividend of 41cps fully franked (A$833 million), including a 21cps final dividend up 62%, its 27th consecutive dividend. The company holds a net cash position with A$1.9 billion liquidity at June 2026 and no debt due until FY29. Brightstar buys Mikado tenements, strikes Panther processing HOA
