Meeka defines maiden 270koz gold resource at Mt Holland

Meeka Metals has reported an initial Mineral Resource of 270koz @ 2.4g/t Au across three deposits, Blue Vein, Bushpig and Razorback, on its 71km2 Mt Holland tenure in the Forrestania greenstone belt. FirstAU to acquire Javelin in recommended all-scrip takeover

First Au has agreed to acquire Javelin under a binding Takeover Implementation Deed, offering 11.7647 FirstAU shares per Javelin share (0.5882 post 20:1 consolidation), implying A$0.1153 per share and valuing Javelin at approximately A$46.5 million. The deal combines Gimlet, Riverina East, Eureka and Coogee into a portfolio holding about 350,600oz of gold near Kalgoorlie. Javelin's Board unanimously recommends the offer, subject to an independent expert's report, with the offer period expected to run from 30 October 2026 to 30 November 2026.

Javelin, MEGA end Eureka right to mine agreement

Javelin Minerals and MEGA Resources have mutually terminated the right to mine agreement covering the Eureka Gold Project near Kalgoorlie, which had included A$25 million in project financing plus geological, engineering, mining and transport services. The parties cited developments around ore processing capacity availability in the Kalgoorlie region. MEGA remains a material shareholder after investing A$1,000,000 in Javelin's September 2025 placement.

STK: New large-scale MT target emerges at Obradov, Rogozna

A newly completed Magnetotelluric survey has defined a large low-resistivity/high chargeability anomaly beneath the Obradov prospect at Strickland's 9.25Moz AuEq Rogozna project in Serbia, coinciding with 2025 drilling that hit carbonate replacement style Pb-Zn-Ag mineralisation suggestive of a porphyry/skarn system. Strickland held $72 million cash and liquids at 30 June 2026, with drill testing of Obradov planned in coming months.

DTM: Dart Mining confirms high-grade tin at historic Walwa mine

Rock chip assays from Dart Mining's June 2026 site visit to the historic Walwa (Mt Alwa) tin mine in Victoria returned up to 3.96% Sn with 165ppm Ta and 197ppm Nb, alongside elevated lithium, rubidium and caesium pointing to a fractionated tin-tantalum pegmatite system. A compilation of historical sampling, including up to 2.91% Sn at Mt Alwa South, supports the targets. Maiden diamond drilling is scheduled to start in October 2026 across nine planned sites.

KZR: Kalamazoo secures 15km gold-antimony corridor at Mt Piper, Victoria

Kalamazoo has consolidated a 100%-owned 1,574km2 exploration position at Mt Piper, including newly granted tenure at Seymour and Hughes Creek giving continuous coverage over a 15km structural corridor. First-pass aircore drilling at Whiteheads Hill (15 holes, 328m) returned up to 155ppm Sb and 68ppb Au, confirming in-situ mineralisation along a west-dipping structure. Rock chips at Kurkuruk returned up to 3.2g/t Au and 1,428ppm Sb over 670m of strike. Next phase work includes hyperspectral imagery and airborne gravity gradiometry to rank targets for staged drill testing.

Day in Brief
 

Corporate and funding: Minerals 260 secured firm commitments for A$250 million (including a A$30 million Franco-Nevada cornerstone) to advance Bullabulling toward FID in early 2027, while First Au agreed a binding all-scrip takeover of Javelin Minerals valuing it at approximately A$46.5 million and creating a combined ~350,600oz Kalgoorlie gold portfolio; Javelin separately terminated its Eureka right-to-mine agreement with MEGA Resources. Emerald Resources signed a US$200 million (scalable to US$400 million) acquisition facility with Sprott and Macquarie to pursue further gold project M&A, alongside ordering two SAG mills for Dingo Range and Memot at a combined circa A$30 million. Gateway Mining confirmed a Listing Rule 15.7 disclosure breach around its earlier A$45 million placement, since completed via a cleansing notice for 562,500,000 shares at A$0.08 each.

Resources and reserves: Meeka Metals lifted group resources to approximately 1.5Moz at 2.8g/t Au on the back of a maiden 270koz Mt Holland resource, with Ore Reserves at 355koz at 2.8g/t Au after depletion; Tolu Minerals grew Tolukuma to 909koz gold (4.31Mt at 6.57g/t Au) including a maiden 199koz Indicated component, lifting total reported gold resources to 1.04Moz with Saki included. Orezone's updated Casa Berardi plan outlined a 14-year, 1,630,000oz mine life at AISC of US$1,877/oz for an after-tax NPV5% of US$1.05 billion, rising to US$1.43 billion at spot gold prices.

Drilling: Barton reported shallow high-grade hits at Tunkillia including 11m @ 8.73g/t Au from 68m, Turaco extended Begnopan's resource footprint with intercepts to 15m @ 3.41g/t Au from 124m, and Pantoro confirmed a 900m high-grade strike at Norseman's Racetrack Discovery including 2.0m @ 24.11g/t Au from 236.66m. Terrace Mining hit bonanza grades at Foroko near the Kobada plant (28m @ 8.86g/t Au from 188m, including 3m @ 61.2g/t), Strickland defined a new large-scale MT target at Obradov within its 9.25Moz Rogozna project, and Alvo secured its environmental licence for Phase 2 drilling at Palma's Touro corridor. Elsewhere, Atomic Eagle extended uranium mineralisation zones at Muntanga North and Namakande in Zambia, Dart Mining confirmed high-grade tin at Walwa in Victoria ahead of October drilling, and Kalamazoo consolidated a 15km gold-antimony corridor at Mt Piper.

Other: US1 Critical Minerals agreed to divest its Mkuju, Foxy and Eland uranium licences to Bradda Head Lithium for up to US$1.8 million while retaining Lilombe, Basin Energy signed a no-cost federally backed research pact with James Cook University on rare earth extraction, and Maritana Minerals quoted shares issued to settle driller invoices. Quarterly filings from Chalice (FY26 net loss A$7.98 million including a A$5.85 million Lynn Lake impairment) and Deep Yellow (FY26 net cash A$160.0 million, debt-free, with Tumas FID delayed) round out the period, alongside routine governance statements.
