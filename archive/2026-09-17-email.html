WGR: Expanded scoping study lifts Gold Duke production target 30%

Western Gold Resources' Expanded Scoping Study for Gold Duke lifts the Production Target 62% to 1.11Mt at 1.7g/t Au for 59.7koz contained gold, up 30% from the September 2025 study's 46.0koz. About 55.5koz is forecast recovered at 93% metallurgical recovery, with 82% of the target from Measured and Indicated resources. At A$6,100/oz gold, the project generates an estimated A$72.9 million operating surplus before capital, financing and tax, with the company now focused on completing the work required to reach first ore.

SLS: New Nanadie diamond tails extend copper-gold well beyond MRE

Three diamond tails at Nanadie returned broad copper-gold zones outside the current 40.4 million tonne resource, led by 52m @ 0.71% Cu, 0.13g/t Au from 406m in NANRCD033, including 6m @ 1.79% Cu, 0.37g/t Au. Follow-up DHEM surveying identified a 140m x 140m conductive plate below the hole near a cross-fault, to be tested immediately by an 850m EIS co-funded hole. Three rigs remain active, with assays pending from 16 diamond and over 20 RC holes.

CZN: Corazon starts maiden RC drilling at Chalice Gold Project

Corazon Mining has commenced a 50-hole, approximately 7,500m RC drill program at its 100%-owned Chalice Gold Project near Higginsville, WA. Drilling targets extensions to the existing 191,000oz at 2.7g/t Au resource, testing continuity up to 1,000m south of the historical open pit and to around 200m vertical depth. The program sits within an Exploration Target of 9.7 to 12.3Mt at 1.9 to 2.1g/t Au (approximately 600,000 to 830,000oz contained gold), and first assays are expected within weeks, with completion due by November 2026.

TAR: Taruga finds new high-grade gold-copper trend at Uaroo Ridge

New soil and rock chip sampling has defined a 500m northeast gold-copper trend cross cutting the existing 1km mineralised corridor at Uaroo Ridge, Gascoyne province, WA. Rock chip UARK042 returned 1.4g/t Au and 4.3% Cu, the highest grade recorded at the prospect to date, alongside 0.5g/t Au and 2.6% Cu (UARK043) and 0.4g/t Au and 2.4% Cu (UARK041).
