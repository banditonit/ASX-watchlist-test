AUE: Boundiali drilling extends high-grade gold beyond resource

Aurum Resources has assayed 60 diamond holes for 12,251.55m at the BMT1 deposit and BMT2 prospect within its 3.22Moz Boundiali Gold Project in Côte d'Ivoire. Best results include 1.35m @ 43.32g/t Au from 17m at BMT1 and 2.38m @ 22.01g/t Au from 277m at BMT2, which sits outside the current Mineral Resource. Results will feed into the next Boundiali Mineral Resource update targeted for early in the December quarter, ahead of a DFS due late CY2026. Aurum held A$54.6 million cash at 30 June 2026.

LEX: Lefroy to weigh Burns inclusion in Mt Martin Scoping Study

Lefroy Exploration is assessing whether to fold the high-grade portion of its Burns gold deposit into the Mt Martin Scoping Study to capture regional processing synergies. The combined study, along with a review of Burns' existing 4.22Mt @ 1.18g/t Au (159,285oz) resource, is now due early in the December 2026 quarter, slipping from the prior timeline. Separately, Miscellaneous Licence L25/71 at Lucky Strike is now fully granted after a Heritage Agreement, with dewatering and mining targeted to resume in the first quarter of 2027.

QML: Land deal unlocks Botos deposit for drilling

CVV: Caravel pushes Caravel Copper DFS back to fourth quarter

Caravel Minerals has rescheduled release of the Definitive Feasibility Study for its 100%-owned Caravel Copper Project to the fourth quarter of calendar 2026, from the September 2026 timing previously guided. The delay covers four workstreams: an eight-week metallurgical programme to lift concentrate grade and recovery and confirm gold and silver recoveries, numerical groundwater modelling of the Yarragadee and Leederville aquifers, an updated Environmental Review Document ahead of EPA assessment, and full review of the process plant capital cost estimate. On release, the company moves into funding and joint venture partnering ahead of a Final Investment Decision targeted for the end of 2027.

Day in Brief
 

Reserves and resources: Tesoro Gold delivered a maiden Ore Reserve of 42.8Mt at 0.93g/t Au for 1.28Moz at Ternera, converting about 87% of the Measured and Indicated Resource and supporting a 13+ year mine life at 3.0Mtpa, with first-decade grade of 1.16g/t Au. Nexus Minerals lifted its combined group Mineral Resource to 14Mt at 1.6g/t Au for 709,000oz, with Wallbrook alone growing 108% to 13.3Mt at 1.5g/t Au for 631,000oz and Crusader-Templar now past half a million ounces.

Drilling and exploration: Aurum Resources reported high-grade gold beyond the current resource at Boundiali, with best results of 1.35m @ 43.32g/t Au from 17m at BMT1 and 2.38m @ 22.01g/t Au from 277m at BMT2, feeding a Mineral Resource update targeted for early in the December quarter. QMines will settle a A$2.388 million land acquisition at Mt Chalmers on 30 October 2026, unlocking drilling at the Botos deposit under an Exploration Target of 1.5 to 2.5Mt @ 0.5 to 0.8g/t Au, with a nine-hole, 1,500m program now underway. Anson Resources cleared an environmental baseline survey at Yellow Cat with no roadblocks identified, paving the way for a Notice of Intent to Drill and Phase 2 twin-hole drilling ahead of a maiden resource.

Studies and timing: Caravel Minerals pushed its Caravel Copper DFS back to the fourth quarter of calendar 2026, citing metallurgical testwork, groundwater modelling, an updated Environmental Review Document and a process plant capex review, with Final Investment Decision still targeted for the end of 2027. Lefroy Exploration is weighing folding the high-grade Burns deposit (4.22Mt @ 1.18g/t Au for 159,285oz) into the Mt Martin Scoping Study, now slipping to early in the December 2026 quarter, while its Lucky Strike Miscellaneous Licence has been fully granted with dewatering targeted for the first quarter of 2027.

Corporate: Barys Resources' renounceable entitlement offer began rights trading under code BRYR, targeting up to A$2.6 million, with trading closing 30 September 2026. Twelve quarterly and annual filings were lodged across the watchlist, none carrying guidance changes.
