PGO: Halted pending a capital raising, days after a high-grade discovery

Pacgold requested a trading halt at 07:32 AWST pending an announcement in relation to a capital raising. The halt runs until the earlier of normal trading on Friday 31 July 2026 or the release of that announcement. The timing follows the 23 July White Dam North result, where WNRC006 returned 20m @ 2.5g/t Au and 0.5% Cu from 58m, including 6m @ 7.5g/t Au and 1.2% Cu from 68m and 3m @ 12.5g/t Au and 1.8% Cu from 68m. WNRC010 returned 10m @ 3.4g/t Au and 0.7% Cu from 54m. Eight of ten RC holes intersected visible copper sulphides with assays still pending, so the announced result is not the full picture. The project sits 80km east of Broken Hill in the Olary Province, South Australia.

 
NST: Guidance revision and a maiden Hemi reserve, inside a routine quarterly

Northern Star revised FY26 production guidance to 1.65Moz at an AISC of A$2,150/oz, and declared a maiden ore reserve of 8.5Moz at Hemi. Neither figure was flagged as price sensitive and both sit inside a document headlined only as a quarterly activities report, which is the reason this briefing reads announcement text rather than headlines.
